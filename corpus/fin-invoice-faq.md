# Supplier Invoice FAQ

Answers to common questions from employees who work with suppliers. For
questions about your own expense claims, see the Expense Claims Policy
instead.

**Where should suppliers send invoices?**
To the Accounts Payable inbox shown on the purchase order. Invoices sent to
you personally are not logged. If one reaches you, forward it to the AP inbox
and tell the supplier to use it next time.

**What must an invoice show?**
The PO number, the company's legal name and GSTIN, the supplier's GSTIN, an
invoice number and date, a description of goods or services that matches the
PO, and the tax broken out by type.

**What happens if there is no PO number?**
AP returns the invoice to the supplier unpaid. The company follows a *no PO,
no pay* rule. If the work was done without a PO, talk to Procurement; a
retrospective PO needs department head approval and is reported to the CFO.

**How does AP decide to pay?**
AP matches three things: the PO, your goods receipt and the invoice. If the
quantity or price differ, the invoice is put on hold and you are notified.

**Why is an invoice on hold?**
The usual reasons are a missing goods receipt, an invoice price above the PO
price, or a quantity greater than you received. The hold reason is shown in
the procurement system. Most holds are cleared by recording the goods receipt
or asking the supplier for a credit note.

**What are the payment terms?**
Standard terms are 45 days from the date AP receives a valid invoice, unless
the contract says otherwise. Payments are released in the weekly supplier
payment run on Thursday.

**Can a supplier be paid early?**
Only with approval from the Finance Controller. Raise a Finance ticket with
the business reason.

**The supplier says they have changed their bank account. What do I do?**
Nothing yourself. Ask them to update the details through the supplier portal.
Procurement verifies every change with a call to a known contact. Treat a
bank change request by email as possible fraud and report it to Finance.

**How do I check whether an invoice has been paid?**
See How to Check a Supplier Invoice's Payment Status.

**Who do I contact?**
Accounts Payable, through a Finance ticket on the employee portal.
