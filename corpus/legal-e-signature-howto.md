# How to Send a Document for E-Signature

Use the company e-signature tool for any agreement that Legal has approved.

1. Open the e-signature tool from the intranet and sign in with single
   sign-on.
2. Choose **New envelope** and upload the final PDF. Do not upload an
   editable Word file.
3. Add the signers in order: the other party first, then our authorised
   signatory.
4. Drag a signature field and a date field onto the signature page for each
   signer.
5. In the message box, add the contract request number from the Legal
   portal.
6. Select **Send**.

When everyone has signed, the tool files the completed copy in the contract
repository. You do not need to upload it yourself.
