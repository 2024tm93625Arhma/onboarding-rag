# Office Parking Policy (v1)

## Purpose
This policy explains how employees at the Bengaluru office get a parking
permit for the office car park.

## Permits
Car parking is available only to employees with a monthly permit. Permits are
requested from the Facilities help desk and are allocated first-come,
first-served.

## Fee
A monthly car parking permit costs **INR 1,000**, deducted from salary. Two-wheeler
parking is free and needs no permit.

## Rules
Park only in marked bays and display the permit at all times. Overnight parking
needs Facilities approval. Reserved bays for employees with disabilities are
allocated on request at no charge.

## Applicability
This policy applies to employees based at the Bengaluru office who
joined before 1 June 2025.
