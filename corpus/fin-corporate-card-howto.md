# How to Get and Use a Corporate Card

A corporate card lets you pay for business costs without using your own money.
The company pays the card bill directly. You still account for every
transaction in the expense tool.

## Who can have one
Employees who travel for work or regularly buy small items for their team.
Your department head decides who needs a card.

## Applying
1. Raise a request in the expense tool under *Cards* > *Apply*.
2. Your department head approves it.
3. Finance orders the card from the bank. It is posted to your office and
   usually arrives within 10 working days.
4. Activate it using the bank's instructions and set a PIN.

Your spending limit is set on the approval and shown in the expense tool.

## Using the card
- Use it only for business costs. Personal spending is not allowed, even if
  you plan to pay it back.
- Keep every itemised receipt.
- Do not withdraw cash unless your approval allows it.
- Do not share the card or its number with anyone, including colleagues.

## Accounting for transactions
Card transactions appear in the expense tool automatically, usually within
two working days. For each one:
1. Open *Card Transactions*.
2. Add the category, business purpose and receipt.
3. Add the transactions to a claim and submit it for approval.

Card transactions follow the same deadline and approval rules as other
claims. These are in the Expense Claims Policy.

## A transaction you do not recognise
Mark it *Disputed* in the expense tool and call the bank on the number on the
back of the card. Finance will follow up with the bank.

## Lost or stolen card
Call the bank at once to block the card, then raise a ticket with Finance so
a replacement can be ordered.

## Leaving the company or changing role
Tell Finance before your last working day. Clear all outstanding
transactions, then cut the card in half and hand it to your manager.
