# Talent Acquisition Team Wiki - Referrals

Working notes for recruiters on handling employee referrals. This page is
for the TA team; employees should use the referral portal.

## How referrals come in
Employees submit referrals through the referral portal with the candidate's
CV and the role they are being referred for. Each referral creates a
candidate record tagged *Referral* in the applicant tracking system.

## Acknowledging the referrer
Send the referrer an acknowledgement within two working days of the referral
arriving. Use the *Referral received* template.

## Screening
Referred candidates go through the same screening as every other candidate.
Do not share interview feedback with the referrer.

## What to tell referrers about the bonus
If they ask, tell referrers that a successful referral earns a bonus of
INR 50,000 per hire, paid once the new joiner has completed their probation.
The referrer must still be employed on the payout date.

## Duplicate referrals
If two employees refer the same candidate, the referral that reached the
portal first counts. Tell the second referrer politely and close their
referral as *Duplicate*.

## Month-end
On the last working day of each month, send Payroll the list of referral
bonuses due that month from the *Referral payouts* report.

## Questions
Ask in the TA team channel or check with the referrals lead.
