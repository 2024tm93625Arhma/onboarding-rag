# Departmental Budget Plan FY 2026-27

**Classification: Restricted. Finance only. Department heads see only their
own line through the planning tool.**

This plan sets the operating and capital budgets for each department for the
financial year from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027, as approved by the Board
on 18 March 2026. It is the reference Financial Planning and Analysis (FP&A)
uses for monthly variance reporting and for any request to move money
between departments.

## Company totals

- **Revenue plan:** INR 1,480 crore, a growth of 22 percent over FY 2025-26.
- **Total operating budget:** INR 1,126 crore.
- **Capital budget:** INR 94 crore.
- **Central contingency:** INR 34 crore, about 3 percent of the operating
  budget, held by the CFO and not allocated to any department.
- **Target operating margin:** 24 percent.

## Headcount plan

The plan allows 312 net new hires across the company. Engineering takes 178
of them, mostly in the payments platform and data teams. Backfills for
leavers do not count against the net new number but need FP&A to confirm
that the role is still in the plan. Any hire above G7 needs CFO approval
whatever the department.

## Phasing

Budgets are phased by quarter in the planning tool. The split for the
company is:

| Quarter | Share of operating budget |
|---|---|
| Q1 (April to June) | 23 percent |
| Q2 (July to September) | 24 percent |
| Q3 (October to December) | 26 percent |
| Q4 (January to March) | 27 percent |

Unspent budget does not carry forward from one quarter to the next unless
FP&A agrees it in writing, and never carries forward into the next
financial year.

## Moving money

- **Within a department:** the department head may move up to INR 50 lakh
  between cost centres in a quarter. Larger moves need FP&A.
- **Between departments:** needs both department heads and the CFO.
- **From contingency:** only the CFO can release contingency. Requests go
  through FP&A with a one-page case. In FY 2025-26, INR 19 crore of the
  contingency was used, mainly for the unplanned data-residency project.

## Variance reporting

FP&A sends each department head a variance report on the eighth working day
of each month. A department that is more than 5 percent over its
year-to-date budget for two months in a row must present a recovery plan at
the next monthly business review. A department more than 10 percent under
budget at the half year may have part of its remaining budget moved to
contingency.

## Assumptions behind the plan

- An average fixed-pay increase of 8.5 percent from 1 July 2026.
- A USD to INR rate of 86.0 for all foreign-currency costs. Every one rupee
  move in the rate changes cloud and software costs by about INR 2.1 crore
  a year.
- Voluntary attrition of 14 percent, with backfills taking an average of 70
  days to join.
- No new office openings beyond the Pune expansion.

## Risks

The main risks FP&A is tracking are a slower revenue ramp in the new
lending product, wage pressure in Engineering above the plan and a weaker
rupee. If revenue in the first half is more than 8 percent below plan, the
CFO will apply the spending controls set out in the Budget Freeze Notice.
