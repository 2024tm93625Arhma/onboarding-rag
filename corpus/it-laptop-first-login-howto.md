# First Login on Your Company Laptop

This guide takes you through the first time you sign in to a new company
laptop, whether you are a new joiner or have received a replacement device.
Allow about thirty minutes, and do it on a reliable internet connection.

## Before you start
- Have your company phone number or personal mobile to hand for multi-factor
  authentication.
- Keep the temporary password from your welcome email. It works only once.
- Plug the laptop into power. Updates run during first login and can drain the
  battery.

## Step 1: Sign in
Turn on the laptop and connect to the network. In the office, the laptop
joins the CORP Wi-Fi by itself. At home, choose your own Wi-Fi network. Sign
in with your company email address and the temporary password.

## Step 2: Set your own password
You are asked to replace the temporary password straight away. Your new
password must meet the current Password Rules policy; read that policy for
the requirements rather than relying on what you used at a previous employer.
We recommend a passphrase of several unrelated words.

## Step 3: Set up multi-factor authentication
Install the authenticator app on your phone when prompted, scan the QR code
shown on screen and approve the test notification. You cannot use company
systems until this step is complete.

## Step 4: Let device management finish
The laptop is already enrolled in device management. After sign-in it
installs security tools, the VPN client and standard applications. A progress
screen is shown; do not restart the laptop until it says setup is complete.

## Step 5: Check your applications
Open email, chat and the intranet to confirm they work. Anything else you
need can be installed from the self-service software catalogue. For software
that is not in the catalogue, follow the request process in the Software
Installation Policy.

## Things not to do
- Do not create a local administrator account.
- Do not sign in with a personal account.

The wider rules on setting up and caring for the laptop are in the Laptop
Request and Refresh Policy.

## If something goes wrong
If a step fails or the laptop shows an error, take a photo of the screen and
contact the IT service desk. New joiners can also visit the IT desk at their
office on their first day.
