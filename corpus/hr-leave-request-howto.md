# How to Apply for Leave

This guide explains how to request leave in the HR system. It does not cover
how much leave you are entitled to or how much you can carry forward; for
those rules, see the Annual Leave Policy version that applies to your join
date.

## Check your balance
1. Open the HR system from the employee portal.
2. Go to *Time Off* > *Balances*. The balance shown already reflects the
   policy version that applies to you.

## Submit a request
1. Go to *Time Off* > *Request*.
2. Choose the leave type, for example annual leave or sick leave.
3. Select the start and end dates. Half days can be selected with the toggle
   next to each date.
4. Add a short note for your manager if the context helps.
5. Submit. Your manager receives an approval task by email and in the HR
   system.

## Plan ahead
Talk to your manager before booking longer breaks, especially around team
deadlines or busy periods. Record planned leave in the shared team calendar
once it is approved.

## Cancelling or changing leave
Open the request under *Time Off* > *My Requests* and select *Cancel* or
*Edit*. Changes to approved leave go back to your manager for approval.

## Sick leave
If you are unwell, tell your manager as early as possible on the day and
record the leave in the HR system when you are able to.

## Leave and working from home
Leave and work-from-home days are recorded separately. A work-from-home day is
not leave; book it as described in the Work From Home Policy.

## Help
If your balance looks wrong, raise a ticket with the HR helpdesk and include a
screenshot of the *Balances* page.
