# IT Service Desk Contacts

The IT service desk handles all questions and problems with company devices,
accounts and software.

## How to reach us
- **IT service portal:** raise a ticket. This is the preferred route and lets
  you track progress.
- **Phone:** call the IT service desk number shown on the intranet home page.
  Use the phone for urgent problems that stop you working.
- **Chat:** post in the #it-help channel for quick questions.
- **In person:** visit the IT desk at your office.

## What to include
Your employee ID, the device asset tag, what you were doing and a screenshot
of any error message.

## Security incidents
Report lost devices or suspected account misuse by phone, not by ticket.
