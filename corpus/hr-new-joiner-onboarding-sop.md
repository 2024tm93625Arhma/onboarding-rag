# New Joiner Onboarding SOP

## Purpose
This procedure sets out how People Operations, IT, Facilities and the hiring
manager work together to onboard a new employee, from accepted offer to the
end of the first month. Its aim is that every new joiner arrives to a working
laptop, the right access, a desk and a manager who is ready for them.

## Scope
The procedure applies to full-time employees, contract employees and interns
joining any office or joining as remote employees. Internal transfers follow
the Internal Transfer procedure instead.

## Roles and responsibilities
- **People Operations (onboarding coordinator):** owns the end-to-end process,
  creates the employee record and tracks every step in the onboarding tracker.
- **Talent Acquisition:** hands over the accepted offer and runs background
  verification as described in the Background Verification SOP.
- **Hiring manager:** prepares the team, chooses a buddy and sets objectives.
- **IT End User Services:** prepares the laptop, accounts and access.
- **Facilities:** prepares the ID badge and, where needed, a parking permit.
- **Payroll:** sets up salary payment once bank details are verified.

## Stage one: offer accepted
1. Talent Acquisition marks the offer as accepted in the applicant tracking
   system. This automatically creates an onboarding case for People
   Operations.
2. The onboarding coordinator checks the offer details: job title, grade,
   department, location, employment type, manager and date of joining.
3. The coordinator creates a pre-joining record in the HR system. The date of
   joining recorded here is used by the HR system to decide which version of
   each HR policy applies to the employee, so it must match the offer letter
   exactly.
4. The coordinator sends the candidate the pre-joining pack: a welcome note,
   the list of documents to bring, and links to complete personal details
   online.

## Stage two: before the first day
1. The coordinator raises the IT onboarding request. IT prepares the laptop
   in line with the Laptop Request and Refresh Policy and creates email,
   chat and HR system accounts.
2. The coordinator asks Facilities to print the ID badge and to arrange a
   parking permit if the joiner has asked for one; the permit terms are in
   the Office Parking Policy.
3. The hiring manager is reminded to complete the tasks in the Manager
   Checklist for New Joiners, including choosing a buddy.
4. The coordinator adds the joiner to the next induction session and sends a
   calendar invitation.
5. The day before joining, the coordinator confirms with IT and Facilities
   that the laptop and badge are ready. Any gap is escalated to the relevant
   team lead the same day.

## Stage three: first day
1. The joiner is greeted at reception, collects their badge and is taken to
   the induction room or joins the induction call.
2. People Operations checks original documents against the copies submitted
   earlier and records the check in the HR system.
3. IT hands over the laptop and helps the joiner sign in, set a password and
   set up VPN access.
4. The joiner completes the First Day Checklist with help from their buddy.
5. The hiring manager meets the joiner and introduces the team.

## Stage four: first month
1. The joiner attends the company induction, which covers company history,
   values, the Code of Conduct, information security and how to find HR
   policies.
2. The joiner completes mandatory e-learning modules in the learning portal.
   The coordinator monitors completion and reminds joiners who fall behind.
3. The hiring manager agrees probation objectives with the joiner and records
   them in the HR system, following the timing in the Probation Policy.
4. Payroll confirms that bank details have been verified before the first
   salary run.
5. At the end of the first month, the coordinator sends a short onboarding
   survey and closes the onboarding case.

## Policy information for joiners
During induction, People Operations explains that several HR policies,
including the Annual Leave Policy, the Work From Home Policy and the
Probation Policy, have more than one version in force at the same time, and
that the version that applies depends on the employee's date of joining.
Joiners are shown how to read the applicability section of a policy. The
induction deliberately does not quote allowances, deadlines or durations from
those policies, to avoid giving out figures from the wrong version.

## Remote joiners
For employees who join remotely, IT ships the laptop to the joiner's
registered address in time for the first day, and the ID badge is issued on
the first office visit. Induction and document checks are done on video.

## Escalation
If any stage is at risk, the coordinator escalates to the People Operations
lead. If a joiner's start date has to move, the coordinator updates the date
of joining in the HR system, informs IT and Facilities, and tells the
candidate in writing.

## Records
The onboarding case, document checks and survey results are kept in the HR
system. The onboarding tracker is reviewed every month by the People
Operations lead to find recurring problems.
