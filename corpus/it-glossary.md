# IT Glossary

Plain-English definitions of IT terms you will see in company guides and
tickets. Where a term is governed by a policy, the policy is named so you can
check the current rules there.

**Asset tag.** The sticker with a barcode on every company device. Quote it in
tickets about hardware.

**Device management.** The service that installs security settings and
software on company laptops and phones, and can lock or wipe them if they are
lost.

**Gateway.** A VPN server you connect to. The VPN client picks the nearest one
when set to *Automatic*.

**Idle timeout.** The period of inactivity after which a session is ended
automatically. For the VPN, the value is set in the VPN Access Policy.

**IT service portal.** The website where you raise tickets and requests for
IT, and track their progress.

**Loaner laptop.** A temporary laptop issued while your own is being repaired
or has not yet arrived.

**Multi-factor authentication (MFA).** Signing in with something you know
(your password) and something you have (an approval on your phone).

**Passphrase.** A password made of several unrelated words. The rules that
passwords must meet are in the Password Rules policy.

**Phishing.** An email or message that pretends to be from someone trusted in
order to steal your credentials or install malware.

**Refresh cycle.** How often a laptop is replaced with a new model. The cycle
is set in the Laptop Request and Refresh Policy and differs between groups of
employees.

**Security key.** A small USB device used as a second factor for highly
privileged accounts.

**Self-service software catalogue.** The app on company laptops for
installing approved software without a ticket.

**Single sign-on (SSO).** Signing in once with your company account to reach
many applications.

**Split tunnelling.** Sending some traffic through the VPN and some directly
to the internet. Whether it is used is set in the VPN Access Policy.

**Ticket.** A record of a request or problem in the IT service portal. Each
ticket has a reference number.
