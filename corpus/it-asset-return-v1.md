# IT Asset Return Policy (v1)

## Purpose
This policy covers the return of company IT equipment when an employee leaves
or when a device is replaced.

## What must be returned
Laptops, chargers, monitors, docking stations, headsets, security keys, mobile
phones and any other equipment issued by IT.

## Return deadline
All equipment must be returned within **7 working days** of the employee's last
working day, or of receiving a replacement device. Equipment can be handed in
at any office IT desk or sent by the courier that IT arranges.

## Before returning a laptop
Sign out of personal accounts and move work files to company storage. Do not
reset or wipe the device; IT does this.

## Unreturned equipment
Final settlement for leavers is held until all equipment is returned.

## Applicability
This policy applies to all employees.
