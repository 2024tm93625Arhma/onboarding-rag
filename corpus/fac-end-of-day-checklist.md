# End of Day Office Checklist

Before you leave the office, run through this list.

- [ ] Clear your desk completely, as the Desk Booking Policy requires.
- [ ] Put personal items in your locker or take them home.
- [ ] Wipe the desk surface with the wipes provided on each floor.
- [ ] Take your laptop with you or lock it in your locker.
- [ ] Collect anything you sent to the printer.
- [ ] Leave meeting rooms tidy and switch off room screens.
- [ ] Put cups and plates back in the pantry.
- [ ] Cancel any desk or room bookings you no longer need for later in the
      week.

Seen something broken? Report it to the Facilities help desk before you go.
