# How to Use Video Conferencing

The company has one approved meeting platform. It is pre-installed on every
company laptop and is linked to your calendar, so meetings you create include
a join link automatically.

## Joining a meeting
Click the join link in the calendar invitation. Check your camera and
microphone on the preview screen, then select *Join now*. If you are joining
from a phone, install the meeting app from your phone's app store and sign in
with your company account.

## Meeting rooms
Every bookable meeting room has a room system with a camera, a microphone bar
and a touch panel. Book the room as part of your calendar invitation. When you
walk in, the panel shows the meeting; tap *Join*. To share your screen from a
laptop, use the share button in the meeting app; cables are not needed.

## Headsets
A headset gives much better audio than a laptop microphone. Accessories are
requested through the IT service portal; the allowance is set in the Laptop
Request and Refresh Policy.

## Recording
Tell participants before you start a recording. Recordings are saved to the
organiser's company storage and follow the company's retention rules. Do not
record meetings about individual employees, such as performance or grievance
discussions.

## External guests
Guests can join from a browser without installing anything. They wait in the
lobby until someone in the meeting lets them in.

## Poor call quality
- Close other applications that use the camera or network.
- Move closer to the Wi-Fi access point, or use a cable.
- Turn off your video if the connection is weak.

## Getting help
For problems with a meeting room system, call the IT service desk from the
room phone. For all other problems, raise a ticket in the IT service portal.
