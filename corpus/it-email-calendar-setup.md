# Setting Up Email and Calendar

Your company mailbox and calendar are created with your account and are ready
on your first day. This page explains how to use them on your laptop and phone.

## On your company laptop
The mail and calendar app is pre-installed. Open it and sign in with your
company email address. Single sign-on completes the setup; there are no
server settings to enter.

## On your phone
1. Install the company-approved mail app from your phone's app store.
2. Sign in with your company email address and approve the multi-factor prompt.
3. When asked, allow the app to apply the company's data protection settings.
   These settings protect work data only and do not give IT access to your
   personal apps or photos.

Built-in phone mail apps are blocked from connecting to company email.

## Email signature
Use the standard signature template on the intranet brand page. Include your
name, job title and office. Do not add images, quotes or personal links.

## Shared mailboxes
Access to a team mailbox is granted by the mailbox owner. Ask the owner to
raise the request in the IT service portal. The shared mailbox appears in
your mail app automatically once access is granted.

## Calendar sharing
By default, colleagues can see when you are free or busy but not the details
of your meetings. You can give a colleague full access, for example an
assistant, from the calendar's sharing settings.

## Out-of-office replies
Set an automatic reply whenever you are on leave. Say when you are back and
who to contact in the meantime.

## Suspicious email
Do not click links or open attachments in unexpected messages. Use the
*Report phishing* button in the mail app, which sends the message to IT
Security and removes it from your inbox.

## Getting help
Raise email and calendar problems through the IT service portal.
