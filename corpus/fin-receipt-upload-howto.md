# How to Attach a Receipt to an Expense Claim

Every claim line needs an itemised receipt. You can add it from your phone or
your laptop.

## From the mobile app
1. Open the expense app and select *Receipts* > *Capture*.
2. Photograph the whole receipt on a flat, well-lit surface.
3. Check that the date, merchant and total are readable, then select *Save*.
4. Open your claim and link the receipt to the matching line.

## From a laptop
1. Open the claim line in the expense tool.
2. Select *Attach* and upload a PDF, JPG or PNG file.

Keep the original paper receipt until the claim is paid. Card slips on their
own are not accepted.
