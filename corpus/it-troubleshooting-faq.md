# IT Troubleshooting FAQ

This FAQ collects the problems the IT service desk hears about most often,
with steps you can try yourself before raising a ticket. Many problems are
fixed by a restart, so try that first unless the answer says otherwise. For
rules and limits, such as password requirements or VPN session limits, this
page points you to the policy that sets them rather than repeating them.

## Accounts and sign-in

### I am locked out of my account.
Accounts lock after several failed sign-in attempts and unlock by themselves
after a short wait. If you need access sooner, call the IT service desk. The
desk will verify your identity before unlocking the account.

### I have forgotten my password.
Use *Forgot password* on the sign-in page. You will be asked to approve a
multi-factor prompt, then to choose a new password. The new password must meet
the Password Rules policy. If you cannot receive the multi-factor prompt, see
the How to Reset Multi-Factor Authentication guide.

### My new password is rejected.
The sign-in page lists the requirements that were not met. The full rules are
in the Password Rules policy. A common cause is reusing a recent password.

### I have a new phone and cannot approve sign-ins.
Follow the How to Reset Multi-Factor Authentication guide.

### Single sign-on keeps asking me to sign in again.
Clear your browser cookies for the company sign-in page, close every browser
window and sign in again. Check that the date and time on your laptop are
correct, because sign-in tokens fail when the clock is wrong.

## Laptop

### My laptop is very slow.
- Restart it. Many laptops are slow simply because they have not been
  restarted in weeks.
- Check that updates have finished installing.
- Close browser tabs you are not using.
- Make sure the laptop has at least some free disk space.

If it is still slow after a restart, raise a ticket and include the asset tag.

### My laptop will not turn on.
Connect it to its own charger for fifteen minutes, then hold the power button
for about ten seconds. Try a different power socket. If it still does not
start, call the IT service desk; a loaner laptop can be issued while yours is
repaired.

### When will my laptop be replaced with a newer model?
The replacement schedule is set in the Laptop Request and Refresh Policy and
depends on your department and join date. IT will contact you when your laptop
is due.

### The screen or keyboard is damaged.
Raise a ticket with a photo of the damage. Do not try to repair the laptop
yourself or take it to an outside repair shop.

### An update is asking me to restart.
Save your work and restart when prompted. You can postpone updates a limited
number of times, after which the laptop restarts automatically.

## Network and VPN

### I cannot connect to the office Wi-Fi.
See How to Connect to Office Wi-Fi. Company laptops should join the CORP
network by themselves.

### The VPN will not connect or keeps disconnecting.
See the VPN Troubleshooting FAQ. Session and inactivity limits are set in the
VPN Access Policy, so a disconnection after a quiet period is expected.

### Internal websites will not open from home.
Connect to the VPN first. The VPN Access Policy lists which systems need it.

## Software

### How do I install an application?
Use the self-service software catalogue. See How to Install Software from the
Catalogue.

### The application I need is not in the catalogue.
Raise a software request through the IT service portal. The approval steps and
the time IT takes to install approved software are set out in the Software
Installation Policy.

### An application keeps crashing.
Check for updates in the software catalogue, restart the laptop and try again.
If the crash continues, raise a ticket with the application name and the time
of the last crash.

### Can I install a browser extension?
See the browser extensions section of the Software Installation Policy.

## Email and meetings

### I am not receiving external email.
Check your junk folder and the quarantine digest email. If a message is in
quarantine, you can release it from the digest if it is safe.

### My camera or microphone does not work in meetings.
Check that the meeting app has permission to use the camera and microphone in
your laptop's privacy settings. Close other applications that might be using
them. If you use a headset, unplug it and plug it in again.

### A meeting room screen will not connect.
Tap *Join* on the room's touch panel rather than connecting a cable. If the
panel is blank, call the IT service desk from the room phone.

## Printing

### My print job does not appear at the printer.
Check that you sent it to the *FollowMe* queue, and that you tapped the same
badge you use every day. See How to Set Up Office Printing.

## Leaving or changing devices

### I have received a new laptop. What happens to the old one?
Hand it back to IT within the time allowed in the IT Asset Return Policy,
which also explains how to prepare it.

### I am leaving the company. What do I need to return?
All company equipment. The list and the deadline are in the IT Asset Return
Policy.

## Still stuck?
Raise a ticket in the IT service portal, or call the IT service desk if you
cannot work at all. The IT Incident Escalation Matrix explains how tickets are
prioritised.
