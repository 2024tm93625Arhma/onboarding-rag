# Sharing Files with TeamVault

TeamVault is our internal file-sharing tool. Use it to store team documents
and to share files with colleagues. Do not use personal cloud storage or
email attachments for work files.

## Getting access
Every employee gets a TeamVault account on their first day. Open
`\\teamvault\home` from File Explorer, or go to the TeamVault web page from
the intranet menu. Sign in with your Windows username and password.

## Your folders
- **Home folder (H: drive):** your personal space. Only you can see it.
- **Team folder (T: drive):** shared with everyone in your team. Your
  manager asks IT to add or remove members.
- **Public folder:** readable by all employees. Use it for templates and
  forms that everyone needs.

The H: and T: drives are mapped automatically when you log in to an office
desktop.

## Sharing a file with someone outside your team
1. Right-click the file in the TeamVault web page and choose *Share link*.
2. Enter the colleague's name and choose *View* or *Edit*.
3. Click *Send*. They get an email with the link.

Links to people outside the company cannot be created. To send a file to a
client or vendor, ask IT for a one-time transfer folder.

## Storage
Each home folder has a fixed quota. When you reach it, TeamVault stops
accepting new files. Delete old files or ask IT for more space through a
HelpLine ticket.

## Backups and restores
TeamVault is backed up every night. If you delete a file by mistake, raise a
HelpLine ticket with the file name and folder, and IT will restore it from
the backup.
