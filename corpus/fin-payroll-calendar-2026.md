# Payroll Calendar 2026

This calendar lists the payroll cut-off and salary payment date for each month
of 2026. It applies to all employees paid through the India payroll.

## How the dates work
- **Cut-off:** the 20th of each month. Anything that changes your pay must be
  approved in the relevant system by the end of that day to be included in
  the same month's salary. This covers new joiner records, bank account
  changes, approved expense claims, leave without pay and salary revisions.
- **Pay date:** salary is credited on the last working day of the month.
  Payslips are published in the HR system on the same day.
- If the pay date turns out to be a public holiday at your bank, the credit
  may show a day later on your statement. The payment is still released on
  the date below.

Approvals that land after the cut-off are processed in the following month's
salary.

## 2026 dates

| Month | Cut-off | Pay date |
|---|---|---|
| January | Tue 20 January | Fri 30 January |
| February | Fri 20 February | Fri 27 February |
| March | Fri 20 March | Tue 31 March |
| April | Mon 20 April | Thu 30 April |
| May | Wed 20 May | Fri 29 May |
| June | Sat 20 June | Tue 30 June |
| July | Mon 20 July | Fri 31 July |
| August | Thu 20 August | Mon 31 August |
| September | Sun 20 September | Wed 30 September |
| October | Tue 20 October | Fri 30 October |
| November | Fri 20 November | Mon 30 November |
| December | Sun 20 December | Thu 31 December |

When the cut-off falls on a weekend, systems stay open and approvals made that
day still count. Payroll staff will not be available to answer queries until
the next working day, so plan any urgent change for the Friday before.

## Year-end dates
- March salary is the last run of the 2025-26 financial year.
- Investment proofs for the 2026-27 financial year are due by the January 2027
  cut-off. Payroll will send a reminder in December.
