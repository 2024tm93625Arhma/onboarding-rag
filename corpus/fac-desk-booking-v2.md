# Desk Booking Policy (v2)

## Purpose
All company offices use shared desks. This policy explains how to book a desk
and the rules for using one.
It replaces v1 for all employees.

## Booking a desk
Desks are booked in the workplace app or on the intranet. Desks can be booked
up to **14 days** in advance. Each booking covers one full working day.

## Neighbourhoods
Each team has a home neighbourhood, an area of the floor where its members are
encouraged to book so that they sit together. Desks outside the home
neighbourhood may be booked if the neighbourhood is full.

## Check-in
Employees must check in to their booked desk by 10:30 using the QR code on the
desk. Bookings not checked in by then are released for others to use.

## Cancelling
Cancel a booking as soon as plans change so the desk becomes available to
others. Repeated no-shows may lead to booking restrictions for one month.

## Clear desk
Desks must be cleared at the end of each day. Personal items may be kept in an
assigned locker; lockers are requested from the Facilities help desk.

## Accessibility
Employees who need an adjusted or fixed desk for medical or accessibility
reasons can request one from Facilities. Such desks are not bookable by others.

## Applicability
This policy applies to all employees. It replaces v1 in full.
