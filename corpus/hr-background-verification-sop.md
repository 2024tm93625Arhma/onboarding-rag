# Background Verification SOP

## Purpose
This procedure describes how Talent Acquisition and People Operations run
background verification for new hires, so that every check is done
consistently and fairly.

## Scope
It applies to all full-time employees, contract employees and interns hired
by the company.

## Roles
- **Recruiter:** collects the candidate's consent and documents.
- **People Operations:** sends the case to the verification vendor and tracks
  it.
- **Hiring manager:** is informed of the outcome but does not see the detailed
  report.

## Procedure
1. When the offer is accepted, the recruiter sends the candidate the
   verification consent form through the candidate portal.
2. The candidate uploads identity proof, address proof, education
   certificates and relieving letters from previous employers.
3. The recruiter checks that the documents are complete and legible, then
   hands the case to People Operations.
4. People Operations opens a case with the approved verification vendor. The
   vendor checks identity, address, education, previous employment and, where
   the role requires it, criminal records.
5. People Operations tracks the case in the HR system and follows up with the
   vendor or candidate on any missing information.
6. When the report arrives, People Operations reviews it and marks the case
   *Clear*, *Clear with remarks* or *Discrepancy*.

## Handling discrepancies
A discrepancy does not automatically mean the offer is withdrawn. People
Operations asks the candidate to explain the difference in writing. The Head
of Talent Acquisition and the HR business partner decide the outcome together,
and the decision is recorded in the HR system.

## Joining before the check is complete
A candidate may join while verification is still in progress if the hiring
manager and HR agree. Their offer letter states that employment is subject to
satisfactory verification.

## Data protection
Verification reports are confidential. They are stored only in the HR system,
visible to People Operations and Talent Acquisition, and deleted in line with
the company's data retention schedule.
