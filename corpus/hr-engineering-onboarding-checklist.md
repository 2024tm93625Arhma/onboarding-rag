# Engineering New Joiner Checklist

Welcome to Engineering. Work through this checklist in your first weeks and
tick items off as you go.

## Before your first day
- [ ] Sign and return your offer letter and the confidentiality agreement.
- [ ] Upload your identity and address documents to the HR portal.
- [ ] Confirm your bank details so your first salary can be paid.

## Week 1
- [ ] Collect your laptop and ID badge from the IT desk.
- [ ] Complete first login and set up multi-factor authentication.
- [ ] Get access to the code repositories and the build system from your
      team lead.
- [ ] Meet your onboarding buddy.
- [ ] Complete the mandatory security awareness course.

## Weeks 2 to 4
- [ ] Ship your first small change to production with your buddy.
- [ ] Join the on-call shadow rota for one week.
- [ ] Read your team's architecture overview and runbooks.

## Your probation
- [ ] Your probation period is 6 months from your joining date. Agree your
      probation objectives with your manager in your first two weeks.
- [ ] Have a check-in with your manager at the midpoint of your probation.
- [ ] Attend your probation review meeting before it ends. Your manager will
      confirm your employment in writing.

## Who to ask
Your onboarding buddy for day-to-day questions, your manager for anything
about your role, and the HR helpdesk for employment matters.
