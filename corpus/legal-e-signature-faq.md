# E-Signature FAQ

Answers to common questions about signing documents electronically. For the
steps to send a document, see How to Send a Document for E-Signature.

**Is an electronic signature legally valid?**
Yes, for most commercial agreements. A few document types still need a wet
ink signature or stamp paper, such as some property documents and powers of
attorney. Legal will tell you when a document needs this.

**Who can sign on behalf of the company?**
Only the authorised signatories listed in the Delegation of Authority
matrix. Signing a contract you are not authorised to sign can make you
personally responsible for it, so do not sign even if the other party asks
you to.

**Can I use a scanned image of my signature instead?**
No. A pasted image does not record who signed or when. Always use the
e-signature tool, which keeps an audit trail.

**The other party wants to use their own e-signature tool. Is that allowed?**
Usually yes. Forward the signing email to Legal Operations first so they can
check the document matches the approved version.

**A signer has not signed. What should I do?**
Use **Remind** in the envelope. If the signer has left the other
organisation, void the envelope and send a new one to the right person.

**I spotted a mistake after sending. Can I edit the envelope?**
No. Void the envelope, fix the document, get it re-approved by Legal if the
change is more than a typo, and send a new envelope.

**Where do I find a signed copy later?**
Search the contract repository by the other party's name or the contract
request number.
