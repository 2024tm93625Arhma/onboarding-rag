# Company Organisation Chart

This page shows who leads each part of the company and who they report to.
Use it to find the right leader for a question or an escalation.

## Leadership team
- **Chief Executive Officer:** Rohan Mehta
- **Chief Operating Officer:** Anita Desai (reports to the CEO)
- **Chief Financial Officer:** Suresh Iyer (reports to the CEO)
- **Head of Technology:** Karthik Rao (reports to the CEO)

## Departments

### Operations (reports to the COO)
- **Head of People and Admin:** Meera Nair. Leads HR, recruitment and
  office administration as one team.
- **Office Manager:** Farhan Sheikh. Looks after the office, reception and
  travel bookings.

### Finance (reports to the CFO)
- **Finance Manager:** Lakshmi Pillai. Leads accounts, payroll and
  expense claims.
- **Legal and Compliance:** handled by an external law firm, coordinated by
  the CFO's office.

### Technology (reports to the Head of Technology)
- **Engineering Manager:** Vikram Singh. Leads all product developers.
- **IT and Infrastructure Lead:** Deepa Krishnan. Looks after desktops, the
  office network, servers and the HelpLine desk.
- **QA Lead:** Arjun Bhat. Leads testing and release sign-off.

## Locations
All teams are based in the Indiranagar office. There are no other offices.

## Changes to this chart
People and Admin updates this chart when a leader joins, leaves or changes
role. Send corrections to Meera Nair.
