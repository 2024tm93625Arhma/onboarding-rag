# Reception Desk FAQ

Common questions for the front desk team at every office.

**How do I get a guest into the building?**
Register your visitor in the visitor management system at least 24 hours
before they arrive. The visitor receives a confirmation email, and reception
prints their badge when they check in.

**What does my visitor need to bring?**
A government-issued photo ID. Reception checks it against the registration
before issuing a badge.

**Do I have to meet my visitor at reception?**
Yes. Reception calls you when your visitor arrives, and you must collect them
and escort them at all times while they are in the building.

**Can visitors connect to Wi-Fi?**
Yes, on the guest network. The access code is printed on the visitor badge.

**What happens to the badge when my visitor leaves?**
Walk your visitor back to reception and make sure they hand in the badge.
Badges not returned are deactivated at the end of the day.

**Can I receive a personal delivery at the office?**
Small parcels only. Reception holds them for a few days and emails you when
one arrives.

**Where is lost property kept?**
At reception. Items are kept for one month.

**What are reception's hours?**
Reception is staffed from 8:00 to 20:00 on working days. Outside those hours,
contact the security desk at the main entrance.
