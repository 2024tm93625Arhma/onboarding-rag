# Checklist for Hosting a Visitor

Use this checklist whenever you invite a candidate, client, vendor or family
member to the office. The rules for registering visitors, including how early
the registration must be made, are in the Visitor Pass Policy. This checklist
helps you plan around them.

## Before the visit
- [ ] Register the visitor in the visitor system, within the deadline set by
      the Visitor Pass Policy.
- [ ] Check that the visitor's name matches their photo ID exactly.
- [ ] Book a meeting room if you need one.
- [ ] Tell the visitor which office and entrance to use and to bring a
      government photo ID.
- [ ] If the visitor needs step-free access or other support, tell reception
      through the visitor system.
- [ ] If the visitor will use a laptop on the network, ask the IT service desk
      about guest Wi-Fi.
- [ ] If the visitor will see confidential material, make sure a
      non-disclosure agreement is in place.

## On the day
- [ ] Be at reception when the visitor arrives.
- [ ] Make sure the visitor wears their pass where it can be seen.
- [ ] Stay with the visitor outside meeting rooms.
- [ ] Keep the visitor out of restricted areas.

## After the visit
- [ ] Walk the visitor back to reception.
- [ ] Make sure the pass is handed in.
- [ ] Clear the meeting room.

## If plans change
Update or cancel the registration in the visitor system so reception is not
expecting someone who will not come.
