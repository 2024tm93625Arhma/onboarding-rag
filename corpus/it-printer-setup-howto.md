# How to Set Up Office Printing

All office printers use secure follow-me printing. You send a job to a single
print queue and collect it from any printer by tapping your ID badge.

## Adding the print queue
1. Open the self-service software catalogue.
2. Install *Follow-Me Printing*.
3. Print as normal and choose the *FollowMe* printer.

## Collecting a print job
Tap your badge on the printer's card reader, select your jobs and press
*Print*. Jobs that are not collected are deleted at the end of the day.

## Problems
If your badge is not recognised, contact the Facilities desk. For any other
printing problem, raise a ticket in the IT service portal.
