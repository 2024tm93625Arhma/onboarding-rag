# Non-Disclosure Agreement Process

This page explains how non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) are prepared,
approved and stored. For the step-by-step request form, see How to Request an
NDA.

## When you need an NDA

You need a signed NDA before you share confidential information with anyone
outside the group. Confidential information includes product roadmaps,
source code, pricing, customer lists, unreleased financial results and
anything marked "Internal" or "Confidential".

You do not need an NDA to:

- share information that is already public, such as our website or press
  releases;
- talk to a supplier about their standard products and published prices;
- speak to a recruiter about a role, as long as you share only the job
  description.

If you are unsure, treat the information as confidential and ask Legal.

## Our templates

Legal keeps two templates:

- **Mutual NDA.** Both sides share information and both are bound. Use this
  for partnerships, joint pitches and most supplier evaluations.
- **One-way NDA.** Only we share information. Use this when a consultant or
  vendor needs to see our material but will not share theirs.

Always start from our template. If the other party insists on their own
paper, submit it through the Legal portal as a contract review request
instead. Do not sign it yourself.

## How a request moves

1. You submit the request in the Legal portal.
2. A Legal Operations analyst checks the other party's details and prepares
   the template.
3. If the other party asks for changes, a lawyer reviews them. Small
   changes, such as the governing law or a notice address, are usually
   accepted.
4. Legal sends the agreement for e-signature. Our side is signed by an
   authorised signatory, not by the requester.
5. The signed NDA is filed automatically in the contract repository.

## After signing

Share only what the project needs, and only with the people named in the
request. Mark documents as confidential when you send them. When the project
ends, ask the other party to return or delete what we shared, and tell Legal
if they refuse.
