# Facilities Escalation Contacts

This page explains who to contact about a building or workplace problem, how
urgent problems are handled and what to do if an issue is not being resolved.
It covers every office. Most problems should start with a ticket to the
Facilities help desk; this page is for the cases where that is not enough.

## Start here

| Situation | Contact | How |
|---|---|---|
| Routine request or minor fault | Facilities help desk | Employee portal ticket under *Facilities* |
| Quick question | Facilities help desk | #ask-facilities chat channel |
| Urgent building fault (leak, power cut, lift stuck, no air conditioning on a floor) | Facilities duty line | Extension 2200, staffed during office hours; calls outside hours divert to the on-call facilities manager |
| Security concern, lost badge, suspicious person, theft | Security | Extension 2222, staffed at all times |
| Fire, medical emergency or injury | Security, then the emergency services | Extension 2222; Security will call the emergency services and guide them to you |

If you are ever unsure whether something is urgent, call. It is better to be
told that a ticket is enough than to leave a hazard in place.

## How urgent problems are classified

When you raise a ticket or call the duty line, Facilities gives the problem a
priority.

- **Emergency.** A risk to people's safety or serious damage to the building,
  such as flooding, a gas smell, electrical sparking or a blocked fire exit.
  Someone is sent immediately and the area is made safe first.
- **Urgent.** A problem that stops a floor or team from working, such as no
  power on a floor, no water in the washrooms or a broken main door.
  Facilities aims to respond the same working day.
- **Routine.** Everything else, such as a broken chair, a flickering light or a
  cleaning request. These are scheduled into the maintenance team's work.

## Escalation path

If a ticket is not moving, escalate in this order. Always reply on the
original ticket first, so the history stays in one place.

1. **Reply on the ticket.** Explain the impact on your work and ask for an
   update. This alerts the assigned technician and the help desk lead.
2. **Office facilities manager.** Each office has a facilities manager who
   owns the building's day-to-day running. Contact them through the *Office
   contacts* list on the intranet *Workplace* page, and quote the ticket
   number.
3. **Regional workplace lead.** If the office facilities manager cannot
   resolve the issue, or the problem affects more than one office, they will
   escalate to the regional workplace lead. You can also ask your manager to
   contact the regional lead directly.
4. **Head of Workplace Services.** For problems that remain unresolved after
   the steps above, or that involve a serious complaint about a vendor, the
   Head of Workplace Services is the final point of escalation.

Security issues follow a separate path: the Security office, then the office
security manager, then the Head of Corporate Security.

## Who owns what

| Area | Owning team |
|---|---|
| Desks, neighbourhoods and lockers | Facilities - Workplace Services |
| Meeting rooms and event spaces | Facilities - Workplace Services |
| Cleaning, pantries and waste | Facilities - Workplace Services (through the cleaning vendor) |
| Building repairs, electrical, plumbing and air conditioning | Facilities - Workplace Services (through the maintenance vendor) |
| Car parks and parking permits | Facilities - Workplace Services |
| ID badges and door access | Facilities - Security |
| Visitors and reception | Facilities - Security |
| CCTV, guards and incident reports | Facilities - Security |
| Monitors, docks, printers and room screens | IT - End User Services |

The policies each team applies, such as the Desk Booking Policy, the Office
Parking Policy and the Visitor Pass Policy, are on the intranet *Policies*
page. This page does not restate their rules.

## Complaints about a vendor

Cleaning, catering, maintenance and security guarding are provided by outside
vendors. If you have a complaint about a vendor's staff or the quality of
their work, raise a ticket under *Facilities > Feedback*. Do not raise it with
the vendor's staff directly. Facilities reviews vendor performance every
quarter and uses these tickets as evidence.

## Out of hours

Outside office hours, the Facilities help desk is not staffed. For a building
fault that cannot wait, call the duty line on extension 2200; it diverts to
the on-call facilities manager. For anything involving safety or security,
call Security on extension 2222. Non-urgent issues found out of hours should be
raised as a ticket and will be picked up the next working day.

## Reporting an incident afterwards

After any emergency, accident or near miss, Security will ask you to complete
an incident report in the employee portal. Include what happened, where, when
and who was involved. Reports are used to prevent the same thing happening
again, not to assign blame.
