# How to Download Your Payslip

Payslips are published in the HR system after each salary run.

## Steps
1. Sign in to the employee portal and open the HR system.
2. Go to *Pay* > *Payslips*.
3. Choose the month you need from the list.
4. Select *Download PDF*. The file is password-protected; the password hint is
   shown on the download screen.

## Something looks wrong?
If a payslip is missing or a figure looks incorrect, raise a ticket with the
Payroll team through the HR helpdesk and attach the payslip. Expense
reimbursements appear as a separate line; for when approved claims are paid,
see the Expense Claims policy.
