# How to Update Your Emergency Contact

Every employee must keep at least one emergency contact on record. HR uses it
only if you are taken ill or injured at work or cannot be reached during an
incident.

## Steps
1. Open the HR system from the employee portal.
2. Go to *My Profile* > *Emergency Contacts*.
3. Select *Add* or edit an existing contact. Enter the person's name,
   relationship to you and a phone number they usually answer.
4. Save your changes. No approval is needed.

Please check your details whenever you move house or your contact changes
their number.
