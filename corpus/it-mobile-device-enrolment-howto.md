# How to Use Work Apps on Your Phone

You can use company email, chat and calendar on your own phone. Company data
on the phone is protected by app protection, which works inside the work apps
only. IT cannot see or wipe your personal apps, photos or messages.

## Requirements
- A phone running a supported operating system version. The list is on the
  intranet IT page.
- A screen lock (PIN, pattern, fingerprint or face).
- The phone must not be jailbroken or rooted.

## Setting up
1. Install the company portal app from your phone's app store.
2. Sign in with your company account and approve the multi-factor prompt.
3. Install the work apps you need: mail, chat and calendar.
4. Sign in to each app. The first time, you are asked to accept the app
   protection settings.

## What app protection does
- You cannot copy work data into personal apps.
- Work apps ask for your PIN or fingerprint after they have been idle.
- If your phone is lost, IT can remove the work data. Personal data is not
  touched.

## Company-issued phones
Phones issued by the company are fully managed. IT can see the installed apps
and can wipe the whole phone if it is lost. Personal use is permitted, but do
not store personal photos or files you would not want to lose.

## Changing or losing your phone
Before you change phones, remove the work apps from the old one. If a phone
with work apps is lost or stolen, report it to the IT service desk the same
day.

## Leaving the company
Work data on your personal phone is removed automatically when your account
is closed. Company-issued phones must be handed back as set out in the IT
Asset Return Policy.
