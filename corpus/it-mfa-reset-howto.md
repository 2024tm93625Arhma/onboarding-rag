# How to Reset Multi-Factor Authentication

Use this guide if you have a new phone, lost your phone, or deleted the
authenticator app.

## If you still have your old phone
Sign in to the account security page on the intranet, choose *Add method*,
register the new phone, and then remove the old one.

## If you no longer have your old phone
Call the IT service desk. You will be asked to verify your identity on a video
call with your photo ID. IT then clears your registered methods, and you set
up the authenticator app again at your next sign-in.

## Lost or stolen phone
Report it to the IT service desk straight away so that the phone can be
removed from your account.
