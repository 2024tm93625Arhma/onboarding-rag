# Contract Review Workflow

Every agreement that commits the company must go through Legal before it is
signed. This page describes the stages a request passes through.

## Stage 1: Intake

Submit a request in the Legal portal under **Contract review**. Attach the
latest draft in an editable format and fill in:

- the other party's full legal name;
- what we are buying, selling or sharing;
- the total contract value and length;
- the business owner who will manage the relationship;
- the date you need it signed, and why.

Requests without a draft or a business owner are sent back.

## Stage 2: Triage

Legal Operations assigns the request a tier:

| Tier | Typical contracts | Reviewed by |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Our own templates with no changes | Legal Operations |
| Negotiated | Our templates with changes, or low-value supplier paper | Commercial lawyer |
| Complex | High-value, multi-year, data-heavy or unusual terms | Senior lawyer, plus Privacy or Security as needed |

## Stage 3: Review and negotiation

The reviewer marks up the draft and returns it to you with notes. You send
the markup to the other party; Legal joins calls when the points are legal
rather than commercial. Each round of changes comes back through the same
request, so keep all versions in it.

## Stage 4: Approval

Once the terms are agreed, the request goes to the approvers set out in the
Delegation of Authority matrix. Finance approves the value; the business
owner's head of department approves the commitment.

## Stage 5: Signature and filing

Legal sends the final version for e-signature. The signed copy is filed in
the contract repository with its renewal and expiry dates, and the business
owner gets reminders before each date.

## Tips

Involve Legal early, especially for anything involving customer data.
Never let the other party start work before the contract is signed.
