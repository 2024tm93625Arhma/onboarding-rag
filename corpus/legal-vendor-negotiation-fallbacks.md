# Vendor Negotiation Fallbacks

**Classification: Confidential. Legal only.**

When a vendor rejects one of our standard positions, a Legal - Commercial
counsel may move to the fallback below without escalating. Anything beyond
the fallback needs the General Counsel. The standard positions themselves
are in Standard Vendor Contract Terms.

## Fallbacks a counsel may agree

- **Liability cap.** The cap may drop to the fees paid in the 6 months
  before the claim, but not below INR 50 lakh in absolute terms. For
  vendors with fees under INR 20 lakh a year, a fixed cap of INR 50 lakh is
  acceptable.
- **Data protection super-cap.** May drop to 2 times the annual fees. It
  may never be removed. Without a super-cap, the contract goes to the
  General Counsel.
- **Payment terms.** Net 30 is acceptable. Anything shorter needs Finance -
  Procurement to confirm the cash impact.
- **Price increases.** A cap of 7 percent a year is acceptable for software
  subscriptions only. Services contracts stay at the standard cap.
- **Termination for convenience.** Notice may go up to 90 days. We may give
  up the pro-rata refund only for fees under INR 10 lakh.
- **Audit rights.** May be replaced by the vendor's current SOC 2 Type II or
  ISO 27001 report, provided we keep the right to audit after a security
  incident.
- **Governing law.** Singapore law with arbitration seated in Singapore is
  acceptable for vendors with no Indian entity. Any other foreign law goes
  to the General Counsel.

## Walk-away points

Legal - Commercial will not sign, and will tell the business to find
another vendor, if the vendor insists on:

- unlimited liability for us with a cap for them;
- the right to use our data to train their own models or products;
- storing employee or customer personal data outside India with no
  transfer safeguards approved by Legal - Privacy;
- automatic renewal with a notice window shorter than 60 days.

## Recording the outcome

Each fallback used is logged in the deviation log with the tag `FB` and the
counsel's name. If the same vendor has needed three or more fallbacks, the
contract is flagged to the General Counsel at the quarterly review even
though no single change needed approval.
