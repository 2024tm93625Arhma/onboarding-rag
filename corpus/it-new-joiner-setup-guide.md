# New Joiner IT Setup Guide

Welcome to the company. This guide takes you through everything IT-related in
your first week: your accounts, your laptop, the tools you will use every day
and where to go for help. It links to the detailed how-to guides and to the IT
policies that set the rules. Where a rule or timeline is set by a policy, this
guide names the policy rather than repeating it, because policies can change
and some apply differently depending on your department or join date.

## Before your first day

Your account is created and your laptop is ordered after you accept your
offer. You will receive a welcome email at your personal
email address a few days before you start. It contains:

- your company email address,
- a temporary password that works only once,
- the address of the office IT desk, or the courier tracking link if your
  laptop is being delivered to your home.

Keep this email safe and do not forward it.

If the welcome email has not arrived two working days before your start date,
ask your hiring manager to contact the IT service desk.

## Day one: accounts and laptop

### Collect your laptop
Office-based joiners collect the laptop from the IT desk. Remote joiners
receive it by courier. Check that the box contains the laptop, the charger and
any accessories listed on the delivery note.

How laptops are requested, which model you receive, what happens if it is
late and when it will be replaced are all set in the Laptop Request and
Refresh Policy.

### Sign in for the first time
Follow First Login on Your Company Laptop. In short:

1. Sign in with your company email and temporary password.
2. Set a new password that meets the Password Rules policy.
3. Set up multi-factor authentication on your phone.
4. Wait for device management to finish installing standard applications.

Do not skip multi-factor setup; you will not be able to use company systems
without it.

### Install the password manager
Install the company password manager from the software catalogue and create
your master password. See How to Use the Company Password Manager. Store every
work password that is not covered by single sign-on in it from the start.

## Day one: everyday tools

### Email and calendar
Your mailbox is ready. Open the mail app on your laptop and it signs in
automatically. To add email to your phone, see Setting Up Email and Calendar.

### Chat
The chat app is pre-installed. Join your team's channels and the company-wide
channels your manager suggests. Useful IT channels are #it-help for questions
and #it-status for outage notices.

### Video meetings
The approved meeting platform is pre-installed and linked to your calendar.
See How to Use Video Conferencing for meeting rooms and headsets.

### Work apps on your phone
If you want work email and chat on your personal phone, follow How to Use Work
Apps on Your Phone. This is optional.

## First week: access and software

### Access to team systems
Your manager requests access to the systems your role needs, such as shared
drives, code repositories or finance tools. Access requests are approved by
the system owner. If you are missing access, ask your manager rather than the
IT service desk, because the manager's approval is needed first.

### Software
Standard software is installed during first login. Other approved software can
be installed at any time from the self-service software catalogue; see How to
Install Software from the Catalogue. For anything not in the catalogue, the
request process and fulfilment time are set in the Software Installation
Policy.

Never install software from outside the catalogue without approval, and never
install tools on the prohibited list in that policy.

### Working from outside the office
To reach internal systems from home, connect to the VPN. The session rules are in the VPN Access Policy, and common
problems are answered in the VPN Troubleshooting FAQ. For a full checklist of
remote working equipment and good practice, see the Remote Working Technology
Guide.

### Printing
If you work in an office, install Follow-Me Printing from the catalogue. See
How to Set Up Office Printing.

### Accessories
You can request accessories through the IT service portal. What you can
request is set in the Laptop Request and Refresh Policy.

## Security basics

- Lock your screen whenever you leave your laptop.
- Never share your password or approve a multi-factor prompt you did not
  start.
- Report suspicious email with the *Report phishing* button.
- Report a lost or stolen device by phone to the IT service desk the same day.
- Complete the security awareness course assigned to you in your first week.

## Getting help

The IT service desk is your first point of contact. See IT Service Desk
Contacts for the ways to reach it, and the IT Glossary if you meet an
unfamiliar term. The IT Troubleshooting FAQ covers the most common problems.
If a problem stops you working, call rather than raising a ticket.

## First week checklist

- [ ] Laptop received and signed in
- [ ] Password changed and multi-factor authentication set up
- [ ] Password manager installed and master password created
- [ ] Email, calendar and chat working
- [ ] VPN tested from outside the office, if you will work remotely
- [ ] Access to team systems confirmed with your manager
- [ ] Security awareness course completed
