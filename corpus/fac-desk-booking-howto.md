# How to Book a Desk

All offices use shared desks, so you need a booking before you come in. This
guide walks through the booking steps in the workplace app. The rules
themselves, including how far ahead you can book and the check-in deadline,
are set out in the Desk Booking Policy. Check that policy if you are unsure
what is allowed.

## Before you start
- Install the workplace app from the company app store on your phone, or open
  the *Workplace* page on the intranet.
- Sign in with your company account. The first sign-in asks you to choose your
  home office.
- Ask your manager which neighbourhood your team uses.

## Booking a desk
1. Open the app and tap **Book a desk**.
2. Pick the date. Dates outside the booking window allowed by the Desk Booking
   Policy are greyed out.
3. Select your office and floor. The floor plan opens with your team's
   neighbourhood highlighted.
4. Tap a green desk to see its equipment, such as a docking station, a second
   monitor or a standing frame.
5. Tap **Confirm**. The booking appears under **My bookings** and in your
   calendar.

## Booking for several days
Use **Repeat** on the confirmation screen to book the same desk on several
dates. Each date is a separate booking and can be cancelled on its own.

## Checking in
When you arrive, scan the QR code on the desk with the app. If you do not
check in by the deadline in the policy, the desk is released to others.

## Cancelling
Open **My bookings**, choose the booking and tap **Cancel**. Cancel as soon as
your plans change.

## Problems
- **The QR code will not scan:** check in from **My bookings** instead and
  report the damaged code to the Facilities help desk.
- **The desk equipment is faulty:** raise a Facilities ticket for furniture or
  an IT ticket for monitors and docks.
- **You need a fixed or adjusted desk:** see the accessibility section of the
  Desk Booking Policy.
