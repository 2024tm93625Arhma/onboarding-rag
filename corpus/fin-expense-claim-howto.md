# How to Submit an Expense Claim

This guide walks you through the expense tool step by step. What you can
claim, the submission deadline and the approval rules are set by the Expense
Claims Policy. Category limits are in the Reimbursement Limits Policy. Check
both before you spend.

## Before you start
- Have an itemised receipt for each expense. A card slip on its own is not
  enough.
- Know your cost centre. The tool fills in your default one; change it only if
  another team's budget is paying.
- For client meals, note the names and organisations of everyone who
  attended.

## Create the claim
1. Open the expense tool from the employee portal.
2. Select *New Claim* and give it a short title, such as *Client visit -
   Chennai - March*.
3. Add one line per expense. For each line:
   - choose the expense category;
   - enter the date shown on the receipt;
   - enter the amount and currency exactly as on the receipt;
   - write the business purpose in one or two sentences;
   - attach the receipt.
4. If you paid in a foreign currency, leave the amount in that currency. The
   tool converts it for you.
5. Check the running total at the top of the claim.
6. Select *Submit*. The claim goes to your line manager.

## Lost receipt
Select *No receipt* on the line and complete the missing-receipt declaration
in the tool. Your manager must countersign it. The Expense Claims Policy
limits how often you can do this.

## After you submit
- You can follow the claim under *My Claims*. The status changes from
  *Submitted* to *Approved* and then *Paid*.
- If your manager returns the claim, read the comment, fix the line and
  resubmit. Do not create a new claim for the same expense.
- Approved claims are paid with your salary. The Payroll Calendar shows the
  cut-off and pay dates. Reimbursements appear as a separate line on your
  payslip.

## Common mistakes
- Two expenses on one line. Split them so each has its own receipt.
- The receipt date does not match the line date.
- A vague purpose, such as *meeting*. Say who you met and why.
- Personal items on a business receipt. Remove them from the amount and say
  so in the purpose.

## Help
For questions about the tool, raise a ticket with Finance through the
employee portal. For questions about whether something can be claimed, see
the Expense Claims Policy or ask your manager.
