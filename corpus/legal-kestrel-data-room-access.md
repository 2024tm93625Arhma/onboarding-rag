# Project Kestrel Data Room Access

**Classification: Restricted. Legal deal team, G6 and above.**

The Kestrel data room is hosted on Vaultline. Access is requested from the
General Counsel's office, not through IT.

- Every document is watermarked with the viewer's name and email.
- Downloading is disabled; printing is allowed only for the financial and
  tax folder.
- The people folder is clean-team only: four named reviewers from HR and
  Finance.
- All access expires on 30 November 2026 unless the deal signs.
- Q&A goes through the data room's question module only, never by email to
  Tessellate.
