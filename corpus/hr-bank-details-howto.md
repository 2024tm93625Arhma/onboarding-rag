# How to Change Your Salary Bank Account

Your salary, expense reimbursements and any bonuses are paid into the bank
account recorded in the HR system. Follow these steps to change it.

## Before you start
- The account must be a savings or salary account in your own name. Joint
  accounts are accepted only if you are the primary holder.
- Have a cancelled cheque or a bank statement showing your name, account
  number and branch code ready as a PDF or clear photo.

## Steps
1. Open the HR system from the employee portal.
2. Go to *My Profile* > *Bank Details* and select *Request Change*.
3. Enter the new account details exactly as they appear on your bank
   document.
4. Upload the cancelled cheque or bank statement.
5. Submit the request. The Payroll team verifies it and you receive a
   notification when it is approved.

## When the change takes effect
If the change is approved before the payroll cut-off, your next salary goes to
the new account. Otherwise it takes effect from the following salary run. Keep
your old account open until you see a payment arrive in the new one.

## Security
Payroll will never ask you to share bank details by chat or email. If you
receive such a request, report it to the IT security team as phishing and do
not reply.

## Problems
If a payment bounces or goes to the wrong account, raise an urgent ticket with
Payroll through the HR helpdesk.
