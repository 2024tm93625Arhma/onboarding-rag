# Budget Freeze Notice H2 FY 2026-27

**Classification: Restricted. Finance only until the CFO announces it.**

First-half revenue closed 9.4 percent below plan, so the CFO has approved a
partial spending freeze from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027:

- New discretionary purchase orders above INR 3 lakh need CFO approval.
- Team offsites and external events are paused.
- 60 of the 134 remaining net new hires are deferred to FY 2027-28.
  Backfills continue.
- INR 8 crore of the Pune fit-out moves to next year.

The CFO will announce the freeze to department heads on 29 September 2026.
