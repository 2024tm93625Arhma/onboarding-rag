# Laptop Request and Refresh Policy (v1)

## Purpose
This policy explains how employees in the Engineering department get a
company laptop, which models are
available, and when a laptop is replaced.

## New joiners
Every new employee receives a company laptop on their first day. The hiring
manager raises the laptop request in the IT service portal when the offer is
accepted, choosing from the standard models below. If the request is raised
late, IT provides a loaner laptop until the standard device arrives.

## Standard models
- **Standard profile:** 14-inch Windows laptop, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD.
- **Developer profile:** 16-inch laptop, 32 GB RAM, 1 TB SSD, available as
  Windows or macOS.
- **Design profile:** 16-inch macOS laptop with a high-colour-accuracy display.

The profile is set by the employee's role. A different profile needs approval
from the department head and a written business justification.

## Refresh cycle
Laptops are replaced on a **4-year** refresh cycle, counted from the date
the device was first issued. IT contacts the employee about two months before
the refresh date to schedule the swap. Employees cannot request an early
refresh simply because a newer model is available.

## Replacement before the refresh date
A laptop is replaced before its refresh date only if:
- it has a hardware fault that IT cannot repair economically,
- it has been lost or stolen and a report has been filed with IT Security, or
- the employee has moved to a role that needs a different profile.

## Accessories
Each employee may request one external monitor, one keyboard, one mouse and one
headset. Docking stations are provided for employees who spend most of their
working week in the office. Accessories are requested through the IT service
portal and are delivered to the employee's office location.

## Setting up the laptop
Laptops arrive pre-enrolled in device management. On first login the employee
must set up multi-factor authentication and set a password that meets the
Password Rules policy. The laptop must not be reset or re-imaged by the
employee.

## Care of the laptop
- Keep the laptop with you or locked away; do not leave it in a vehicle.
- Do not install unapproved software (see the Software Installation policy).
- Report damage, loss or theft to the IT service desk the same day.

## Old devices
When a laptop is replaced, the old device must be handed back to IT within the
timeline in the Asset Return policy so it can be wiped and recycled. Old
devices may not be kept or purchased by employees.

## Applicability
This policy applies to employees in the Engineering department who
joined before 1 October 2024.

## Contact
Raise laptop questions through the IT service portal or the IT service desk.
