# Travel Desk FAQ

Answers to the questions the Travel Desk is asked most often. For anything
not covered here, raise a ticket under *Finance > Travel*.

**How do I book a business trip?**
Submit a travel request in the travel portal with your dates, destination and
cost centre. Once your manager approves it, the Travel Desk books your
tickets and hotel and sends the itinerary to your work email.

**How early do I need to book a domestic flight?**
Domestic flights must be booked at least 14 days before the travel date. If
you cannot meet that, add a short justification to the request and your
manager must approve the late booking.

**Can I book my own tickets and claim them back?**
Only in an emergency, and only with written approval from the Travel Desk.
Keep the booking confirmation and the invoice for your claim.

**Which hotels can I stay in?**
Use the preferred hotels listed in the travel portal. They have negotiated
rates and direct billing, so you do not pay at checkout.

**Can I choose my airline or seat?**
The Travel Desk books the lowest reasonable fare. You can state a preferred
airline in the request, but it is not guaranteed. Seat selection charges are
not covered.

**What if my plans change?**
Tell the Travel Desk as soon as you know, through the same ticket. Changes
made directly with the airline or hotel may not be refunded.

**Do I need travel insurance?**
Company travel insurance covers all approved business trips. The policy
certificate is attached to your itinerary email.

**Who do I call if something goes wrong while travelling?**
The Travel Desk emergency line printed on your itinerary is staffed around
the clock.
