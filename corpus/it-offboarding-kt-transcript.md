# End User Services KT Session - Leaver Equipment

Transcript of a knowledge transfer session for new End User Services team
members on handling equipment from employees who are leaving. Lightly edited.

**Presenter:** When HR confirms a resignation, a leaver ticket is created
automatically and lands in our queue. It lists every asset registered to the
person: laptop, monitors, headset, phone, security key.

**Attendee:** Do we contact the leaver straight away?

**Presenter:** Yes. Send the *Leaver equipment* email template the same day.
It tells them what they need to hand back and where.

**Attendee:** And the deadline?

**Presenter:** Everything has to be returned within 7 working days of their
last working day. Office-based people usually drop things at the IT desk on
their last day. Remote people get a prepaid courier box.

**Attendee:** What if something is missing when it comes back?

**Presenter:** Record it on the ticket and tell HR. Missing items are
recovered from the final settlement, so HR needs to know before payroll
closes the person's account.

**Attendee:** Do we wipe the laptop right away?

**Presenter:** No. Hold it for thirty days in case the manager needs data
recovered. After that, wipe it, check its condition and put it back into
stock or send it for disposal.

**Attendee:** What about their accounts?

**Presenter:** Identity and Access disable those on the last working day. We
only handle the hardware.

**Presenter:** Questions? Otherwise that is leavers done.
