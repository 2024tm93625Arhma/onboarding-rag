# Bengaluru Office Floor Guide

This guide describes the layout of the Bengaluru office so that new joiners
and visiting colleagues can find their way around. The office occupies the
ground floor and floors 1 to 5 of the building. Floor plans with desk numbers
are available in the workplace app and on the intranet *Workplace* page. The
rules for booking desks, parking and hosting visitors are not repeated here;
see the Desk Booking Policy, the Office Parking Policy and the Visitor Pass
Policy.

## Getting in

The main entrance faces the service road and is open to employees with an ID
badge from early morning until late evening. Outside those hours, use the
side entrance next to the Security cabin, where the guard will check your
badge. The car park entrance is at the rear of the building and leads to the
basement levels. A ramp and a lift connect the basement to the ground floor
lobby, so the whole route is step-free.

## Ground floor

- **Reception.** Straight ahead as you enter. Reception issues ID badges to
  new joiners, temporary day badges and visitor passes.
- **Facilities desk.** To the left of reception. Go here for lockers, locker
  keys, *Do not use* tags and general questions about the building.
- **IT service desk walk-in counter.** To the right of reception. Collect
  laptops and get help with device problems here.
- **Security office.** Behind reception. This is a restricted area; knock at
  the counter window.
- **Cafeteria.** At the back of the ground floor, serving breakfast and lunch
  on working days. The menu is posted weekly in the #blr-office channel.
- **Showers and changing rooms.** Beside the cafeteria, with lockers for
  cyclists and runners.
- **Wellness room.** Next to the changing rooms. It can be used for rest,
  prayer, nursing or taking medication, and is booked from the calendar like
  a meeting room.

## Floor 1: Training and events

Floor 1 holds the town hall space, three training rooms and a large pantry.
These rooms are booked through a Facilities events ticket rather than the
calendar. When no event is running, the floor is open as a quiet overflow
area with lounge seating but no bookable desks.

## Floor 2: HR, Finance and Legal

Floor 2 houses the People Operations, Payroll, Finance and Legal teams. The HR
desk for walk-in questions is next to the lift lobby. Part of this floor is a
restricted zone that holds personnel files; your badge will not open it unless
you work in one of those teams. Four small interview rooms near the lifts are
used for candidate interviews and confidential conversations.

## Floors 3 and 4: Engineering and Product

These are the largest open-plan floors, divided into team neighbourhoods
marked by coloured signs hanging from the ceiling. Each floor has:

- a pantry with a coffee machine, a fridge and a microwave,
- a print room next to the fire exit stairs,
- phone booths along the window side for one-person calls,
- a mix of small, medium and large meeting rooms named after Indian rivers.

The server room and the network room are on Floor 4, behind a badge-controlled
door. Only IT staff and approved engineers can enter.

## Floor 5: Sales, Support and leadership

Floor 5 holds the Sales, Customer Support and Marketing teams, along with the
leadership offices and the boardroom. The boardroom is booked through the
executive assistants rather than the calendar. The Customer Support area runs
extended hours, so parts of this floor stay lit and staffed into the evening.
Please keep noise down near the support team's area, as they are often on
calls with customers.

## Every floor

- **Fire exits** are at both ends of each floor and are marked with green
  signs. Stairwells lead directly to the assembly point in the front car park.
- **First aid kits** are in every pantry, and an automated external
  defibrillator is next to the lifts on the ground floor and Floor 3.
- **Accessible washrooms** are next to the lifts on every floor.
- **Recycling bins** are in every pantry: blue for paper, green for food
  waste and grey for everything else.
- **Lost property** is handed in at the Facilities desk and kept for a short
  period before being donated.

## Quiet and focus areas

Library-style quiet zones are marked with a purple sign on Floors 3, 4 and 5.
Calls, video meetings and conversations are not allowed there. If you need to
take a call, move to a phone booth or a meeting room.

## Getting help

For anything about the building, contact the Facilities help desk. For urgent
problems such as leaks or power failures, call the Facilities duty line on
extension 2200. For emergencies, call Security on extension 2222. The
Facilities Escalation Contacts page lists who to contact if an issue is not
being resolved.
