# First Day Checklist

Use this list on your first day and tick each item off as you go.

- [ ] Collect your ID badge from the reception desk.
- [ ] Collect your laptop from the IT service desk and sign the asset form.
- [ ] Set a new password that meets the IT Password Rules policy.
- [ ] Set up remote access as described in the IT VPN Access policy.
- [ ] Complete your personal, bank and emergency contact details in the HR
      system.
- [ ] Read and acknowledge the Code of Conduct in the employee portal.
- [ ] Meet your manager and your onboarding buddy.
- [ ] Join your team's chat channels and the #new-joiners channel.
- [ ] Book a desk as described in the Desk Booking Policy.

Stuck? Ask your buddy or the HR helpdesk.
