# How to Find Your Cost Centre

You need a cost centre code for expense claims, corporate card transactions
and purchase orders. It tells Finance which team's budget pays for the cost.

## Finding your default code
1. Open the HR system and go to *My Profile* > *Job Details*.
2. Your cost centre is shown under *Organisation*, for example `CC-4120`.

The expense tool fills in this code automatically.

## Charging another team
If the cost belongs to a different team or project, ask that team's budget
holder for their code and enter it on the claim line. Their budget holder
will then be asked to approve the line.
