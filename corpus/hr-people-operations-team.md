# People Operations Team

People Operations runs the day-to-day HR services that every employee relies
on, from your first day to your last.

## What we look after
- **Onboarding:** offer paperwork, background verification, first-day
  logistics and induction sessions.
- **Employee records:** personal details, job changes, transfers and
  employment letters.
- **HR policies:** drafting, publishing and answering questions on HR policies
  such as leave, probation and work from home.
- **HR systems:** the HR system, the attendance system and the employee portal
  HR pages.
- **Exits:** resignation processing, exit clearance and relieving letters.

## What we do not handle
- Payroll queries go to the Payroll team.
- Benefits and insurance claims go to Total Rewards.
- Workplace concerns and grievances go to Employee Relations.
- Laptops, accounts and access go to IT through the IT service portal.

All of these teams can be reached through the HR helpdesk; choose the right
category when you raise a ticket and it will be routed automatically.

## How we work
We aim to answer every helpdesk ticket on the day it arrives and keep you
updated if something takes longer. We publish changes to HR policies on the
employee portal and announce them in the #announcements channel. When a
policy changes, older versions stay available for employees they still apply
to, so always check the applicability section of a policy.

## Office presence
A People Operations partner is available at the HR desk in each office.
Remote employees can book a video call through the HR helpdesk.

## Feedback
Tell us how we are doing through the short survey sent when a ticket closes,
or write to the Head of People Operations directly through the helpdesk.
