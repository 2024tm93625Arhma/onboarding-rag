# How to Check a Supplier Invoice's Payment Status

If a supplier asks when they will be paid, you can check the invoice yourself
before contacting Accounts Payable.

1. Open the procurement system from the employee portal.
2. Go to *My Purchase Orders* and open the purchase order.
3. Select the *Invoices* tab. Each invoice shows one of these statuses:
   - *Received* - logged by AP, not yet matched.
   - *On hold* - a mismatch needs your attention; read the hold reason.
   - *Approved* - scheduled for the next supplier payment run.
   - *Paid* - the payment date and reference are shown.

If the invoice is not listed at all, AP has not received it. Ask the supplier
to resend it to the AP inbox with the PO number on it.
