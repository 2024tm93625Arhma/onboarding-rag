# Reimbursement Limits Policy (v1)

## Purpose
This policy sets the maximum amounts the company reimburses for common
business expenses. How to submit claims is covered by the HR Expense
Claims policy.

## Meals during domestic travel
While travelling on approved business within India, employees may claim meals
up to **INR 1,500** per day. The limit is per day, not per meal, and covers
breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks. Meals included in a hotel rate or provided
at an event cannot also be claimed.

## Hotels
Nightly hotel limits depend on the city tier and are published by the Travel
Desk. Stays above the limit need prior written approval from the department
head.

## Client entertainment
Client meals are reimbursed at actual cost with a business purpose and attendee
list. Expenses of this kind that exceed the amount set by Finance each year
need department head approval.

## Local conveyance
Taxis and ride-hailing for business travel within a city are reimbursed at
actual cost. Use of a personal vehicle is reimbursed at the per-kilometre rate
published by Finance.

## Internet and phone
Employees who are required to work from home or travel regularly may claim
internet and mobile costs up to the monthly amount set for their role.

## Items not covered
Alcohol, minibar charges, laundry on trips shorter than five nights, and
personal entertainment are not reimbursed.

## Applicability
This policy applies to all employees.
