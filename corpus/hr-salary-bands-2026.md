# Salary Bands by Grade 2026-27

**Classification: Restricted. HR only. Do not forward, print or paste into
tickets, chat or email outside the HR function.**

This document sets the fixed-pay salary bands for every grade for the
2026-27 financial year. It is used by Talent Acquisition when making offers,
by HR Business Partners when reviewing promotions and transfers, and by
Total Rewards during the annual compensation review. Employees and managers
outside HR are told only their own band, or the bands of their direct
reports, through the compensation module of the HR system.

## Grade structure

The company uses nine grades. G1 to G5 are individual contributor grades.
G6 is the first manager grade, and "manager grade and above" in any
restricted document means G6 to G9.

| Grade | Title | Track |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | Associate | Individual contributor |
| G2 | Senior Associate | Individual contributor |
| G3 | Specialist | Individual contributor |
| G4 | Senior Specialist | Individual contributor |
| G5 | Principal | Individual contributor |
| G6 | Manager | Management |
| G7 | Senior Manager | Management |
| G8 | Director | Management |
| G9 | Vice President | Management |

A G5 Principal who moves into people management is re-graded to G6 only if
the role has at least four direct reports. Otherwise the employee stays at
G5 with the Principal title.

## Bands

Figures are annual fixed pay (basic, HRA and flexible allowances together)
in INR lakh. They exclude variable pay, employer PF and gratuity.

| Grade | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | 4.8 | 6.2 | 7.6 |
| G2 | 7.2 | 9.6 | 12.0 |
| G3 | 11.4 | 14.9 | 18.4 |
| G4 | 16.5 | 21.3 | 26.1 |
| G5 | 24.2 | 31.1 | 38.0 |
| G6 | 29.5 | 37.8 | 46.1 |
| G7 | 41.0 | 53.4 | 65.8 |
| G8 | 58.6 | 77.2 | 95.8 |
| G9 | 88.0 | 116.5 | 145.0 |

Bands overlap on purpose. A strong G4 can be paid more than a new G5.

## Engineering premium

Roles in the Engineering department at grades G2 to G6 carry a market
premium of 14 percent on top of the band. The premium applies to the
minimum, midpoint and maximum alike, so the G4 Engineering midpoint is
INR 24.3 lakh. The premium does not apply at G1, where campus hires are
paid the same across departments, or at G7 and above, where pay is set
individually against the executive benchmark.

## Variable pay targets

Variable pay is paid once a year after the annual review. Targets are a
percentage of annual fixed pay:

- G1 to G3: 8 percent
- G4 and G5: 11 percent
- G6 and G7: 15 percent
- G8: 20 percent
- G9: 25 percent

The payout multiplier depends on the final performance rating and the
company scorecard. The multiplier table is in the Compensation Review Cycle
2026 document.

## Compa-ratio

Compa-ratio is an employee's fixed pay divided by the midpoint of their
band. It is the main measure Total Rewards uses to judge whether someone is
paid fairly for their grade.

| Compa-ratio | Position | What it usually means |
|---|---|---|
| Below 0.85 | Low in band | New to grade, or underpaid |
| 0.85 to 1.05 | In range | Fully competent in the role |
| 1.05 to 1.20 | High in band | Long-serving or consistently strong |
| Above 1.20 | Above band | Red-circled; see below |

## Rules for offers

- New hires are offered between the band minimum and the midpoint. An offer
  above the midpoint needs written approval from the HR Business Partner and
  the department head. An offer above a compa-ratio of 1.10 also needs the
  Chief People Officer.
- An offer may never exceed the band maximum. If a candidate's expectation
  is above the maximum, Talent Acquisition must check whether the role has
  been graded correctly before going back to the candidate.
- Joining bonuses are capped at 20 percent of annual fixed pay and are
  clawed back in full if the employee leaves within twelve months of
  joining.

## Rules for promotions

A promotion moves the employee to the next grade with a fixed-pay increase
of at least 10 percent. If that still leaves the employee below the new
band minimum, pay is raised to the minimum. Promotion increases are capped
at 25 percent unless the Chief People Officer approves more.

## Red-circled employees

An employee whose pay is above the band maximum is red-circled. Their fixed
pay is not cut, but they receive no merit increase to fixed pay while they
remain above the maximum. They may instead receive a one-time lump sum,
described in the Compensation Review Cycle 2026 document. At the start of
this financial year, 37 employees were red-circled, most of them at G3 in
Finance after the 2025 band correction.

## Review of the bands

Total Rewards benchmarks the bands every January against two salary surveys
and adjusts them from 1 April. For 2026-27, the bands moved up by an average
of 6.8 percent, with G3 and G4 moving most because of market pressure on
mid-level hiring. The next benchmark is due in January 2027.

## Access and handling

This document is available to the HR function only. HR Business Partners may
share a single band with a manager who is making an offer or a promotion
case for that grade. Sharing the full table outside HR, or with an employee,
is a breach of the Information Classification Standard and is handled under
the Disciplinary Procedure. Questions go to the Total Rewards team.
