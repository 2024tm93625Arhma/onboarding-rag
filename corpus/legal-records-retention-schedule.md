# Records Retention Schedule

This schedule sets how long each type of company record is kept and what
happens to it afterwards. It applies to records in every format: paper,
email, files in shared drives and data in business systems. For general
questions, see the Records Retention FAQ.

## How to read the schedule

- **Retention period** is the minimum time a record is kept. It starts from
  the trigger event shown, not from the date the record was created.
- **Owner** is the team responsible for applying the period and approving
  deletion.
- **At end of period** says whether the record is deleted, anonymised or
  reviewed.

A legal hold always overrides this schedule. If a record is under hold, keep
it until Legal releases the hold in writing, even after its period ends.

## Corporate records

| Record | Retention period | Trigger | Owner | At end of period |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Certificate of incorporation, articles and memorandum | Permanent | - | Company Secretary | Keep |
| Board and committee minutes and resolutions | Permanent | - | Company Secretary | Keep |
| Statutory registers | Permanent | - | Company Secretary | Keep |
| Annual returns and regulatory filings | 10 years | Filing date | Company Secretary | Delete |

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Supplier invoices and payment records | 8 years | End of financial year | Finance - Accounts Payable | Delete |
| Tax returns and assessment orders | 10 years | End of assessment | Finance - Controllership | Review |
| Bank statements | 8 years | End of financial year | Finance - Controllership | Delete |

## Contracts and legal records

| Record | Retention period | Trigger | Owner | At end of period |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Signed contracts and amendments | 8 years | Expiry or termination | Legal - Commercial | Review |
| Non-disclosure agreements | 5 years | Expiry of the confidentiality term | Legal - Commercial | Delete |
| Patents, trademarks and IP assignments | Permanent | - | Legal - Commercial | Keep |
| Litigation files | 10 years | Final judgment or settlement | Legal | Review |
| Conflict-of-interest declarations | 5 years | Date of declaration | Legal - Compliance | Delete |

## Customer and operational records

| Record | Retention period | Trigger | Owner | At end of period |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customer account data | 2 years | Account closure | Business owner | Anonymise |
| Customer support tickets | 2 years | Ticket closure | Business owner | Anonymise |
| Marketing consent records | 5 years | Withdrawal of consent | Marketing Operations | Delete |
| System audit logs | 1 year | Log date | IT - Network Security | Delete |
| CCTV footage | 90 days | Recording date | Facilities - Security | Delete |

## Deleting records

At the end of each quarter, owners review records that have reached the end
of their period. Deletion must be secure:

- Electronic records are deleted through the system's own purge function, not
  by moving them to a recycle bin.
- Paper records go into the locked confidential waste bins, which are
  shredded on site by an approved vendor.
- For records marked **Review**, the owner decides whether there is a
  business or legal reason to keep them longer and records that decision.
  If there is none, the record is deleted.

Keep a deletion log listing the record type, the date range deleted and who
approved it. Do not list the personal data itself in the log.

## Records not in this schedule

If you hold a type of record that is not listed, keep it and raise a
question in the Legal portal under **Records and retention**. Compliance will
add it to the schedule at the next review.

## Review of this schedule

Legal - Compliance reviews this schedule once a year and whenever the law
changes. Owners are told of any change to their record types.
