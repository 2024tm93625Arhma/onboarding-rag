# How to Request an Employment Letter

You may need a letter confirming your employment for a visa application, a
loan, a rental agreement or a similar purpose. People Operations issues these
letters on company letterhead.

## Letter types
- **Employment verification letter:** confirms your role, department and
  date of joining.
- **Salary certificate:** also states your current annual compensation.
- **Address proof letter:** confirms your address as recorded in the HR
  system.
- **Visa support letter:** for business or personal travel, addressed to the
  relevant embassy.

## Steps
1. Open the employee portal and go to *HR Services* > *Letters*.
2. Choose the letter type.
3. Enter the purpose and, if required, the name and address of the
   organisation the letter should be addressed to.
4. Submit the request. You receive the signed letter as a PDF by email.

## Things to check first
Make sure your name, address and job title in the HR system are correct.
Letters are generated from those records and cannot be edited by hand.

## Original signed copies
If an organisation needs a physical copy with a wet signature, say so in the
purpose field and collect it from the HR desk at your office.

## Help
For urgent or unusual requests, raise a ticket with the HR helpdesk.
