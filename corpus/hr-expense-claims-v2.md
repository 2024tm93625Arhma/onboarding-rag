# Expense Claims Policy (v2)

## Purpose
This policy explains how employees claim back business expenses they have paid
for personally, what evidence is required, and how claims are approved and
paid.
It replaces v1 for all employees.

## Scope
The policy covers business expenses incurred while carrying out work for the
company, including client meals, local conveyance, work-related travel costs,
conference fees and small purchases made on the company's behalf. Spending
limits for specific categories such as meals, hotels and per-diems are set out
in the Finance Reimbursement Limits policy and are not repeated here.

## What can be claimed
- Local travel for work purposes, such as a taxi to a client site or between
  company offices during the working day
- Meals with clients or partners where a business purpose is recorded
- Registration fees for approved conferences, courses or certifications
- Stationery, cables or other small items bought for work when the standard
  procurement route was not practical
- Mobile data or roaming charges incurred during approved business travel

## What cannot be claimed
- Personal entertainment, gifts for colleagues or celebrations
- Fines, parking penalties or traffic violations
- Travel between home and your usual office location
- Upgrades to flights or hotel rooms that were not pre-approved
- Expenses that have already been paid by a client or a third party

## Submission deadline
All expense claims must be submitted in the expense tool within
**30 calendar days** of the date the expense was incurred. Claims submitted after
this deadline are rejected automatically and cannot be reopened, except where
the employee's manager and the Finance Controller both approve a written
exception.

## How to submit a claim
1. Log in to the expense tool from the employee portal.
2. Create a new claim and select the correct expense category.
3. Enter the amount, the date and the business purpose in one or two sentences.
4. Attach a clear photo or scan of an itemised receipt. Card slips alone are
   not accepted.
5. For client meals, list the names and organisations of all attendees.
6. Submit the claim for approval.

## Receipts
An itemised receipt is required for every expense. If a receipt is lost, the
employee may submit a signed missing-receipt declaration, which the manager
must countersign. Missing-receipt declarations may be used no more than twice
in a calendar year; further lost receipts mean the expense cannot be claimed.

## Approval
Claims are approved by the employee's line manager. Claims above the threshold
defined in the Reimbursement Limits policy also need approval from the
department head. Managers are expected to review claims within five working
days of submission. A manager may return a claim for correction; a returned
claim keeps its original expense date for the purpose of the submission
deadline.

## Payment
Approved claims are paid through payroll in the next salary run after
approval, provided approval is completed before the payroll cut-off on the
20th of the month. Claims approved after the cut-off are paid with the
following month's salary. Reimbursements are not taxable when they are
supported by valid receipts.

## Foreign currency expenses
Expenses paid in a foreign currency are claimed in the original currency. The
expense tool converts the amount to Indian rupees at the exchange rate on the
date of the expense. If the employee paid with a personal card, a card
statement showing the rupee amount charged may be attached instead, and the
statement amount is reimbursed.

## Corporate cards
Employees who hold a corporate card must still record each card transaction in
the expense tool with a receipt and business purpose. Corporate card
transactions follow the same submission deadline as personal expense claims.

## Audit and misuse
Finance audits a random sample of approved claims every quarter. Submitting a
false, duplicated or inflated claim is treated as a disciplinary matter under
the Code of Conduct and may lead to recovery of the amount paid.

## Applicability
This policy applies to all employees. It replaces v1 in full.

## Contact
Questions about this policy can be raised with the HR helpdesk through the
employee portal.
