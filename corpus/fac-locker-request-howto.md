# How to Request a Locker

Because desks are shared and cleared every evening, you can keep personal
items in an assigned locker.

1. Open the employee portal and raise a ticket under *Facilities > Lockers*.
2. Choose your office and, if you have one, a preferred floor.
3. Facilities assigns a locker and replies on the ticket with its number.
4. Collect the locker key from the Facilities desk, or tap your ID badge on
   the locker if it has a badge reader.

Lockers are for personal use only. Do not store food overnight, company
laptops you are not carrying home, or anything hazardous.

If you lose a locker key or move to another floor, raise a new ticket so the
locker can be reassigned.
