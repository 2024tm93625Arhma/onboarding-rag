# Visitor Pass Policy (v1)

## Purpose
This policy explains how employees register visitors to company offices.

## Pre-registration
Every visitor must be pre-registered by their host in the visitor system at
least **24 hours** before arrival. The visitor receives a QR code by email.

## On arrival
Visitors show the QR code and a government photo ID at reception and receive a
visitor pass. The host must meet the visitor at reception.

## During the visit
Visitors must wear the pass visibly and be accompanied by the host at all times
outside meeting rooms. Visitors may not enter restricted areas such as server
rooms.

## Departure
Passes are returned at reception when the visitor leaves.

## Applicability
This policy applies to all employees.
