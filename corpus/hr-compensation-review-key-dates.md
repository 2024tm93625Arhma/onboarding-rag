# Compensation Review 2026: Key Dates

**Classification: Confidential. Managers at G6 and above, and HR.**

- **27 April:** merit budgets open in the HR system.
- **22 May:** manager recommendations lock at 18:00 IST.
- **5 June:** department heads and HR Business Partners finish their review.
- **19 June:** Chief People Officer and CFO sign off.
- **29 June:** letters released to employees in the HR system.
- **1 July:** new fixed pay takes effect.
- **July payroll:** variable pay for FY 2025-26 is paid.

Late recommendations are entered by the HR Business Partner at the
guideline midpoint.
