# HR Helpdesk Contacts

The HR helpdesk is the first point of contact for any question about your
employment, pay, benefits or HR policies.

## How to reach us
- **Employee portal:** raise a ticket under *HR Helpdesk*. This is the fastest
  route and keeps a record of your request.
- **Chat:** post in the #ask-hr channel for quick, non-confidential questions.
- **In person:** an HR desk is staffed at each office during office hours.

## What to include
Your employee ID, a short description of the issue and any documents that
help explain it.

## Confidential matters
For personal or sensitive concerns, choose the *Confidential* ticket type.
Only the Employee Relations team can see these tickets.
