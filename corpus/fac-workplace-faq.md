# Workplace and Office Facilities FAQ

This FAQ answers the questions new joiners most often ask about working in a
company office. Where a question is covered by a Facilities policy, the answer
points you to that policy rather than repeating it, because policies change
and the policy is always the authoritative source.

## Desks and seating

**Do I have a permanent desk?**
No. All offices use shared desks that you book in the workplace app. The How
to Book a Desk guide explains the steps.

**How far in advance can I book a desk?**
The booking window is set by the Desk Booking Policy. The workplace app only
lets you pick dates inside that window.

**What is a neighbourhood?**
An area of a floor set aside for one team so that its members can sit
together. Your manager can tell you which neighbourhood is yours.

**Can I leave my things on the desk overnight?**
No. Desks must be cleared at the end of every day. Use a locker for anything
you want to keep in the office. The End of Day Office Checklist lists what to
do before you leave.

**I need a special chair or a fixed desk for a medical reason.**
Raise a confidential ticket under *Facilities > Workplace adjustments*. The
Desk Booking Policy explains how adjusted and fixed desks work. An occupational
health assessment may be arranged for you.

## Meeting rooms and quiet spaces

**How do I book a meeting room?**
Add a room to a calendar invitation. The How to Book a Meeting Room guide has
the details, including how to book large rooms and event spaces.

**Where can I take a private call?**
Use a phone booth or a small meeting room. Phone booths do not need a booking
and are available on a first-come basis.

**Is there a quiet area?**
Yes. Quiet zones are marked with a purple sign. Calls and conversations are
not allowed there.

## Getting to the office

**Is there parking?**
Parking depends on your office. Where car parking is available, it needs a
permit under the Office Parking Policy, and the Parking and Commuting FAQ
explains how to apply. Check the policy for the charge that applies to you.

**Can I cycle to work?**
Yes. Bicycle racks, showers and changing rooms are available at most offices.
See the floor guide for your office.

## Visitors

**Can I bring a guest to the office?**
Yes, if they are registered in the visitor system in line with the Visitor
Pass Policy. The Checklist for Hosting a Visitor walks you through the steps.

**Can my family visit me at the office?**
Yes, under the same visitor rules as any other guest. Children must be
supervised at all times and cannot enter the pantries, print rooms or
restricted areas.

## Food and pantries

**Is food provided?**
Offices with a cafeteria serve breakfast and lunch on working days. Every
floor has a pantry with tea, coffee and drinking water at no cost.

**Can I store food in the pantry fridge?**
Yes, labelled with your name and the date. Fridges are cleared every Friday
evening and anything left inside is thrown away.

**Can I eat at my desk?**
Light snacks are fine. Please eat full meals in the cafeteria or pantry to
keep shared desks clean for the next person.

## Health, safety and the environment

**What do I do if the fire alarm sounds?**
Leave by the nearest fire exit, do not use the lifts and go to the assembly
point shown on the floor plan. Fire wardens in high-visibility vests will
guide you. Do not go back into the building until a warden tells you it is
safe.

**Where is the first aid kit?**
In every pantry. The names of trained first aiders are posted beside each
kit.

**How do I recycle?**
Use the colour-coded bins in the pantries. Printer toner cartridges and
batteries go to the collection box at the Facilities desk.

**The office is too cold.**
Temperature is set centrally for each zone. If a zone is consistently too hot
or too cold, raise a maintenance ticket rather than adjusting the controls
yourself.

## Personal items and property

**I lost something in the office.**
Check with the Facilities desk, where lost property is kept. If you think
something was stolen, report it to Security.

**Can I have a plant or a photo on my desk?**
Not on a shared desk, because it has to be clear at the end of the day. Small
items can be kept in your locker. Teams may put plants in their neighbourhood
with Facilities approval.

**Can I charge an electric scooter or vehicle at the office?**
Personal electric scooters and e-bike batteries must not be charged indoors
because of the fire risk. Some car parks have charging points; ask the
Facilities help desk.

## Getting help

**Who do I contact about the office?**
The Facilities help desk, through a ticket or the #ask-facilities channel.
For urgent faults call the duty line on extension 2200, and for emergencies
call Security on extension 2222. The Facilities Escalation Contacts page
explains what to do if a problem is not being fixed.
