# Password Rules (v2)

## Purpose
This document sets the rules for passwords on company accounts.
It replaces v1 for all employees.

## Password requirements
- Minimum length: **14 characters**.
- Must include at least one letter and one number.
- Must not contain your name, username or the company name.
- Must not match any of your last five passwords.

Passphrases made of several unrelated words are encouraged.

## Expiry
Passwords do not expire on a schedule. You must change your password
immediately if you suspect it has been exposed, or if IT Security asks you to.

## Storing passwords
Use the company password manager. Do not write passwords down or share them,
including with IT staff.

## Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication is required on all company accounts in addition to
the password.

## Applicability
This policy applies to all employees. It replaces v1 in full.
