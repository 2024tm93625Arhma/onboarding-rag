# How to Book a Meeting Room

Meeting rooms are booked through your calendar, just like inviting a person.

## Booking a room
1. Create a new meeting in your calendar.
2. Select **Add room** and filter by office, floor and capacity.
3. Tick any equipment you need, such as a video conferencing screen or a
   whiteboard.
4. Choose a free room and send the invitation. The room accepts
   automatically if it is free.

If the room declines, someone else has booked it. Pick another room or time.

## Checking in
Each room has a panel by the door. Tap **Check in** on the panel within
15 minutes of the start time. Rooms that are not checked in are released so
others can use them.

## Ending early
If your meeting finishes early, tap **End meeting** on the panel so the room
shows as free.

## Large rooms and event spaces
Rooms for more than 20 people, the town hall space and the training rooms
cannot be booked from the calendar. Raise a ticket under *Facilities > Events*
and describe the event, the number of attendees and any catering or
audio-visual support you need.

## Good practice
- Book the smallest room that fits your group.
- Do not hold recurring bookings you no longer use.
- Leave the room as you found it: wipe the whiteboard, take your rubbish and
  put chairs back.
- Hosting external guests? Register them as described in the Visitor Pass
  Policy before you book the room.

## Problems
Report a broken screen, camera or speaker to the IT service desk. Report
furniture, lighting or temperature problems to the Facilities help desk.
