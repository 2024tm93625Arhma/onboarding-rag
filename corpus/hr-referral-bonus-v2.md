# Employee Referral Bonus Policy (v2)

## Purpose
This policy rewards employees who refer candidates that the company goes on to
hire.
It replaces v1 for all employees.

## Bonus amount
A successful referral earns a bonus of **INR 75,000** per hire, paid to the
referring employee. The amount is the same for all roles and levels.

## Eligibility
- The referral must be submitted through the referral portal before the
  candidate applies through any other channel.
- The referred candidate must not have applied to the company in the
  preceding six months.
- Hiring managers, recruiters and HR business partners are not eligible for a
  bonus on roles they are directly hiring for.
- Referrals of former employees are eligible only if they left the company
  more than twelve months earlier.

## Payment
The bonus is paid through payroll in the salary cycle after the referred hire
completes probation. Both the referring employee and the new hire must still
be employed on the payment date. The bonus is taxable income.

## Duplicate referrals
If more than one employee refers the same candidate, the bonus goes to the
first referral recorded in the portal.

## Applicability
This policy applies to all employees. It replaces v1 in full.
