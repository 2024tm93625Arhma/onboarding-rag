# How to Raise a HelpLine Ticket

HelpLine is the ticketing system for IT and admin requests. Raise a HelpLine
ticket for any problem with your desktop, email, network drives, printers or
phone, and for requests such as new software or access to a TeamVault
folder.

## Raising a ticket
1. Open HelpLine from the *Quick Links* box on the intranet home page, or
   go to `http://helpline.corp.local`.
2. Log in with your Windows username and password.
3. Click *Log a Call*.
4. Choose a category: *Hardware*, *Software*, *Network*, *Access* or
   *Admin*.
5. Write a one-line summary and a description of the problem. Include your
   desktop's computer name from the asset sticker.
6. Click *Submit*. You get an email with your call number, starting *HL-*.

## By email or phone
If you cannot open HelpLine, email `helpline@corp.local`; the message is
turned into a ticket automatically. For urgent problems that stop you
working, call extension 222.

## Tracking your ticket
Click *My Calls* to see your open tickets. The status moves from *Logged* to
*Assigned*, *In Progress* and *Closed*. You can add a note to a ticket at any
time; the engineer is notified.

## Priorities
- **P1:** many people cannot work, for example the network is down.
- **P2:** you cannot work at all.
- **P3:** something is broken but you can keep working.
- **P4:** a request, such as new software or a new folder.

Choose P1 only after calling extension 222.

## Closing a ticket
When the engineer marks a ticket *Resolved*, you get an email. Reply
*reopen* if the problem is not fixed; otherwise the ticket closes
automatically.
