# New Starter Office Checklist

A checklist to help you settle into the office in your first couple of
weeks.

## Getting in
- [ ] Collect your ID badge from reception on your first day.
- [ ] Test your badge on the main entrance and your floor's door.
- [ ] Note the emergency exits and assembly point for your floor.

## Finding a place to work
- [ ] Install the workplace app on your laptop and phone.
- [ ] Book a desk in the workplace app. You can book a desk up to 7 days in
      advance, and each booking covers one full working day.
- [ ] Find your team's neighbourhood on the floor plan so you can sit near
      them.
- [ ] Check in at your desk through the app by 10:30, or the booking is
      released.

## Around the office
- [ ] Find the kitchen, the quiet rooms and the wellbeing room.
- [ ] Request a locker if you want to leave things overnight.
- [ ] Learn how to book a meeting room from your calendar.

## Before you leave each day
- [ ] Clear your desk. Anything left behind is removed by the cleaning team.
- [ ] Lock your laptop away or take it home.

## Help
For anything to do with the building, raise a ticket with the Facilities
help desk.
