# Standard Vendor Contract Terms

**Classification: Confidential. Legal only.**

These are the company's standard positions for contracts with vendors and
service providers. Legal - Commercial uses them when drafting from our
template or marking up a vendor's paper. They are not shared with vendors or
with the business, because doing so would show our negotiating range. For
the review workflow itself, see the Contract Review Workflow.

## Positions we start from

| Term | Standard position |
|---|---|
| Limitation of liability | Each side's total liability capped at the fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the claim |
| Data protection super-cap | 3 times the annual fees for breaches of data protection or confidentiality |
| Uncapped | Fraud, wilful misconduct, IP infringement indemnity, death or personal injury |
| Payment terms | Net 45 days from receipt of a valid invoice |
| Price increases | Capped at 5 percent a year or the CPI change, whichever is lower |
| Term | Initial term of up to 3 years |
| Renewal | No automatic renewal; renewal only by written agreement |
| Termination for convenience | By us, on 60 days' written notice, with a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees |
| Termination for breach | On 30 days' notice if the breach is not cured |
| Service credits | At least 10 percent of monthly fees for each full percentage point below the availability target |
| Governing law | Laws of India; arbitration seated in Bengaluru |
| Audit rights | Once a year on 15 business days' notice, and at any time after a security incident |
| Subcontracting | Only with our prior written consent |
| Data location | Personal data stored in India unless Legal - Privacy approves otherwise |

## Terms that always need the General Counsel

Some changes cannot be approved by a Legal - Commercial counsel alone and
must go to the General Counsel:

- any liability cap below the standard position;
- any indemnity we give to a vendor;
- exclusivity or minimum purchase commitments;
- governing law outside India;
- a term longer than 3 years.

The fallback positions a counsel may agree without escalation are set out in
the Vendor Negotiation Fallbacks document.

## Insurance

Vendors handling personal data or with access to our systems must hold
cyber liability insurance of at least INR 10 crore per claim. Vendors
providing on-site staff must hold public liability insurance of at least
INR 5 crore.

## Using these terms

Mark up the vendor's paper against this table, not against memory. Record
every deviation in the deviation log in the contract register, with the
reason and who approved it. Deviations are reviewed by the General Counsel
every quarter.
