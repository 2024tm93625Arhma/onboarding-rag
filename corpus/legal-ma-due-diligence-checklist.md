# M&A Due Diligence Checklist: Project Kestrel

**Classification: Restricted. Legal deal team only. Insider information:
do not trade in the securities of any party, and do not discuss the deal
outside the deal team.**

Project Kestrel is the proposed acquisition of 100 percent of Tessellate
Analytics Private Limited, a Hyderabad-based fraud analytics company with
about 140 employees. The non-binding letter of intent was signed on 14
August 2026 at an indicative enterprise value of INR 410 crore, subject to
due diligence. Exclusivity runs until 30 November 2026.

This checklist sets out what the deal team must review, who owns each
workstream and the thresholds for what counts as material. It is used with
the virtual data room; access rules for the data room are in Project
Kestrel Data Room Access.

| Role | Owner |

## Materiality thresholds

Report an item to the deal lead as material if it meets any of these:

- a contract worth more than INR 25 lakh a year, or any contract with a
  change-of-control clause;
- a dispute, claim or regulatory inquiry with exposure above INR 10 lakh;
- any customer contributing more than 5 percent of Tessellate's revenue;
- any finding that could reduce the enterprise value by more than 2
  percent, or about INR 8 crore.

## Workstream checklists

### Corporate and legal

- [ ] Certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association,
      and all amendments.
- [ ] Shareholding pattern and a full cap table, including ESOP pool,
      convertible notes and investor rights (liquidation preferences,
      anti-dilution, drag-along and tag-along).
- [ ] Board and shareholder minutes for the last five years.
- [ ] Statutory registers and filings with the Registrar of Companies.
- [ ] Foreign investment filings, if any foreign shareholder holds shares.
- [ ] Litigation, arbitration and regulatory notices, current and threatened.

### Commercial contracts

- [ ] All customer contracts above the materiality threshold, with a
      summary of term, pricing, liability caps and termination rights.
- [ ] Every change-of-control and assignment clause, and whether consent is
      needed for the deal.
- [ ] Exclusivity, non-compete and most-favoured-customer clauses that
      would bind the combined business.
- [ ] Top 20 vendor contracts, including cloud hosting and data providers.

### Intellectual property

- [ ] Registered and pending trademarks and patents.
- [ ] IP assignment agreements for every current and former employee and
      contractor who wrote core code. Gaps here are a closing condition.
- [ ] Open-source licence scan of the main codebase. Any copyleft licence in
      a distributed product must be reported as material.
- [ ] Ownership of the fraud-scoring models and the training data used.

### Data protection and security

- [ ] Data maps showing what personal data Tessellate holds, where it is
      stored and on what basis it is processed.
- [ ] Data processing agreements with its bank and fintech customers.
- [ ] Security incidents and breaches in the last three years, and any
      notices given to CERT-In, regulators or customers.
- [ ] Most recent penetration test and SOC 2 or ISO 27001 reports.
- [ ] Whether any customer data is stored outside India.

### Financial and tax

- [ ] Audited financial statements for the last three years and management
      accounts for the current year.
- [ ] Quality of earnings review by the external accounting firm.
- [ ] Tax returns, assessments and open tax demands, including GST.
- [ ] Debt, guarantees and off-balance-sheet liabilities.
- [ ] Revenue recognition for multi-year contracts.

### People

- [ ] Organisation chart and a list of all employees with grade-equivalent,
      fixed pay, variable pay and notice period, anonymised until the
      clean-team stage.
- [ ] Key person list. The founders and the eight senior data scientists
      named in the LOI must sign retention agreements before closing.
- [ ] ESOP plan terms and the effect of the deal on vesting.
- [ ] Pending HR disputes, harassment complaints and statutory compliance
      (PF, ESI, gratuity, shops and establishments registrations).

### Regulatory

- [ ] Whether the deal needs Competition Commission of India approval under
      the deal value threshold.
- [ ] Any licences or registrations Tessellate holds with the Reserve Bank
      of India or other regulators, and whether they transfer.

## Red-flag reporting

Anything likely to change the price, the structure or the decision to
proceed goes to the deal lead within one business day of being found, by
phone, not email. The red-flag report groups findings as *deal breaker*,
*price adjustment*, *indemnity or escrow* and *post-closing fix*. The LOI
allows for an escrow of up to 12 percent of the purchase price for 18
months.
