# Employee Benefits FAQ

This page answers common questions about the benefits the company offers. The
Total Rewards team maintains it. Some benefits are governed by versioned HR
policies whose terms depend on your join date; for those, this page tells you
where to look instead of quoting the terms.

## Health insurance

### Am I covered by health insurance?
Yes. All employees are covered by the company's group health insurance. When
cover starts for employees on probation is stated in the Probation Policy.

### Can I add my family?
You can add your spouse or partner, your children and either your parents or
your parents-in-law. Add dependants in the HR system under *Benefits* >
*Dependants*. Changes are accepted when you join, during the annual enrolment
window and within a short period after a life event such as marriage or the
birth of a child.

### How do I make a claim?
For planned hospital treatment, request pre-authorisation through the
insurer's app or website before admission. For emergencies, the hospital
contacts the insurer directly using your e-card. For treatment at a hospital
outside the insurer's network, pay the bill, keep all original documents and
submit a reimbursement claim through the insurer's app.

### Where is my insurance e-card?
Download it from the insurer's app once your enrolment is processed. A copy is
also available in the HR system under *Benefits*.

### Can I buy more cover?
Yes. You can buy a top-up policy for yourself and your dependants during the
annual enrolment window. The premium is deducted from your salary.

## Life and accident cover

### Is there life insurance?
All employees have group term life cover and group personal accident cover.
Nominees are recorded in the HR system; please keep them up to date.

## Retirement benefits

### Do I contribute to the provident fund?
Yes. Provident fund contributions are made by you and the company as required
by law. You can see your contributions on your payslip and track your balance
on the government portal using your universal account number, which is shown
in the HR system.

### What about gratuity?
Gratuity is paid on leaving in accordance with the law. People Operations can
confirm your eligibility when you resign.

## Leave-related benefits

### How much annual leave do I get?
See the Annual Leave Policy. Entitlement and carry-forward rules differ by
join date, so read the version that applies to you.

### Is there parental leave?
Yes. Maternity, paternity and adoption leave are available. Speak to People
Operations as early as you are comfortable so that we can plan cover and
explain your options.

## Wellbeing

### Is there an employee assistance programme?
Yes. The employee assistance programme offers free, confidential counselling
for you and your immediate family by phone, chat or video. The contact details
are on the benefits page of the employee portal. The company is not told who
uses the service.

### Does the company support fitness?
Employees can join partner gyms at a corporate rate. Details are on the
benefits page of the employee portal.

## Learning

### Will the company pay for courses or certifications?
Approved courses and certifications can be funded. Get your manager's
approval before you register. If you pay the fee yourself, claim it back as
described in the Expense Claims Policy.

## Flexible working

### Can I work from home?
Yes, within the limits of the Work From Home Policy. The weekly allowance
depends on your join date.

## Referral bonus

### Do I get anything for referring a friend?
Yes. The amount and conditions are in the Employee Referral Bonus Policy.

## Changes to benefits

### Can benefits change?
The company reviews benefits every year. Changes are announced on the employee
portal before they take effect, and this FAQ is updated at the same time.

### Who do I contact with questions?
Raise a ticket with the Total Rewards team through the HR helpdesk. For
questions about a specific insurance claim, contact the insurer's helpline
first; Total Rewards can help if a claim is stuck.
