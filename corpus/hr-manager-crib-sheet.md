# Manager Crib Sheet - New Team Members

Quick answers for managers to the questions new team members ask most. Keep
it open during your first one-to-one with a new joiner.

## Where they work
**Can I work from home?**
Yes. New team members can work from home for up to 3 days per week once they
have completed their first two weeks in the office. The days are agreed with
you and marked in the attendance system by Friday of the previous week.

**Do they need to be in on particular days?**
Yes, on the team's anchor days. Tell them which days those are in the first
week.

## Their first weeks
**Who sets up their laptop and access?**
IT. You raise the new joiner request and IT has everything ready on day one.

**Who is their buddy?**
Pick someone from the team before they start and introduce them on day one.

**When should I set objectives?**
In the first two weeks. Keep them to three or four and write them down.

## Pay and admin
**When is salary paid?**
On the last working day of each month. Payslips appear on the HR portal the
same day.

**Where do they update personal details?**
On the HR portal. You do not need to approve these changes.

## Getting help
Your HR business partner for anything about the employee, the HR helpdesk for
employment admin, and the IT Service Desk for equipment.
