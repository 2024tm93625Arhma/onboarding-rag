# How to Declare a Conflict of Interest

Declare a conflict as soon as you notice it, even if you are not sure it
counts.

1. Open the Legal portal and choose **Declare a conflict of interest**.
2. Select the type: outside job, family relationship, financial interest,
   gift or other.
3. Describe the situation in plain words and name the people or companies
   involved.
4. Add your manager as the reviewer.
5. Submit the form.

Your manager and the Compliance team review the declaration and record an
agreed action. Until you hear back, step back from any decision that the
conflict could affect.
