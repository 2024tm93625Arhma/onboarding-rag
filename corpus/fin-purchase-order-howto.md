# How to Raise a Purchase Order

A purchase order (PO) is the company's formal commitment to pay a supplier.
You need an approved PO before you place any order with a supplier. The only
exception is a small purchase of up to INR 10,000 paid on a corporate card.
For the full process from quote to payment, see the Procure-to-Pay Guide.

## Before you start
- The supplier must already be set up in the procurement system. If they are
  not, start vendor onboarding first; see the Vendor Onboarding Checklist.
- Have the supplier's quote as a PDF. Orders above INR 5,00,000 need three
  quotes, or a written reason why only one supplier can do the work.
- Know the cost centre that will pay.

## Steps
1. Open the procurement system from the employee portal.
2. Select *Create* > *Purchase Requisition*.
3. Choose the supplier and enter a clear description of what you are buying.
4. Add one line per item or service, with quantity, unit price and delivery
   date.
5. Enter the cost centre and attach the quote or quotes.
6. Submit the requisition. It is routed for approval automatically.
7. Once it is approved, Procurement converts it into a PO and sends it to the
   supplier. You receive a copy by notification.

## Who approves
| Order value | Approvers |
|---|---|
| Up to INR 5,00,000 | Budget holder for the cost centre |
| Above INR 5,00,000 up to INR 25,00,000 | Budget holder and department head |
| Above INR 25,00,000 | Budget holder, department head and CFO |

## After the PO is sent
- Give the PO number to the supplier contact and ask them to quote it on
  every invoice. Invoices without a valid PO number are returned unpaid.
- When the goods or services arrive, record a goods receipt against the PO
  under *My Purchase Orders* > *Receive*. Accounts Payable cannot pay the
  invoice until you do.

## Changing or cancelling a PO
Open the PO and select *Request Change*. A change that raises the value goes
back through approval. To cancel, select *Cancel* and give a reason;
Procurement tells the supplier.
