# Records Retention FAQ

Quick answers about keeping and deleting company records. The periods for
each type of record are in the Records Retention Schedule.

**What is a record?**
Any information created or received in the course of business that shows
what the company did or decided. Contracts, board minutes, approved budgets
and signed forms are records. Drafts, duplicates and chat about lunch are
not.

**Where should records be kept?**
In the system of record for that type: the contract repository for
contracts, the HR system for employee files, the finance system for
accounting documents. Personal drives and inboxes are not systems of record.

**Why can't I just keep everything?**
Keeping data longer than needed breaks data protection law, costs money to
store and makes searches during a dispute slower and riskier.

**Can I delete a record early?**
No. Records must be kept for the full period in the schedule, even if they
seem no longer useful.

**What is a legal hold?**
An instruction from Legal to keep certain records because of a dispute,
investigation or audit. A legal hold overrides the schedule: do not delete
anything covered by it, even if its period has ended. You will receive an
email naming the hold, and you must confirm it.

**What happens to my files when I leave?**
Your manager reviews your drive before your account is closed and moves any
records into the right system. Anything left is deleted with the account.

**Who do I ask about a record that is not in the schedule?**
Raise a question in the Legal portal under **Records and retention**.
