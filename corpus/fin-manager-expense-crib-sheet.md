# Manager Crib Sheet - Approving Team Expenses

A one-page reference for managers approving their team's claims in the
expense tool. When in doubt, send the claim back with a question rather than
approving it.

## What to check on every claim
- The expense was for business, not personal, purposes.
- A receipt is attached for every line.
- The cost centre is your team's.
- Nothing has been claimed twice.

## Meals on domestic business trips
The daily meal allowance is INR 1,500 per day on domestic trips. It is a
daily limit, not a per-meal limit. Return any claim over the limit unless the
employee has explained why, for example a client dinner.

## Hotels and local transport
Hotels should be booked through the Travel Desk and billed directly, so they
should not appear on a claim. Taxis and ride-hailing within the city are
fine for business journeys; ask about anything unusual.

## Alcohol and entertainment
Alcohol is not reimbursed unless it was part of approved client
entertainment. Client entertainment needs the client's name on the claim.

## Things you cannot approve
- Claims for yourself.
- Claims from outside your team.
- Anything that looks like a gift to another employee.

## Help
Ask the Accounts Payable team through a Finance ticket.
