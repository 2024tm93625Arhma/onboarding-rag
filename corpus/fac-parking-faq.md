# Parking and Commuting FAQ

This FAQ covers practical questions about getting to the office. Permit
eligibility, charges and car park rules are set by the Office Parking Policy.
Which version of that policy applies to you depends on your office and join
date, so check the policy for the details that apply to you.

**How do I apply for a car parking permit?**
Raise a ticket under *Facilities > Parking* and give your vehicle
registration number. Permits are allocated in the order requests arrive. You
will be told on the ticket when yours is ready to collect.

**How much does a permit cost?**
See the Office Parking Policy. The charge is deducted from salary, and the
amount depends on which version of the policy governs you.

**Can I park a motorcycle or scooter?**
Yes. Two-wheeler parking is in the marked area at the car park entrance. The
Office Parking Policy sets out how it works.

**Is there bicycle parking?**
Yes. Bicycle racks are by the main entrance and are covered by CCTV. Showers
and changing rooms are on the ground floor.

**I have a disability. Can I get a reserved bay?**
Yes. Raise a ticket under *Facilities > Parking* and choose *Accessible bay*.
Your request is kept confidential.

**Can I leave my car overnight?**
Only with approval from Facilities. Ask on a ticket before the day.

**I have changed my car. What do I do?**
Update the registration number on your permit through a Facilities ticket.

**Is there a company shuttle?**
Some offices run a shuttle from the nearest metro station during peak hours.
Timings are posted on the intranet *Workplace* page.

**Where can visitors park?**
Visitor parking is limited. Mention it when you register your visitor under
the Visitor Pass Policy and reception will try to reserve a space.

**Who do I ask about something not covered here?**
The Facilities help desk.
