# Exit Interview Summary H1 2026

**Classification: Confidential. HR only. Individual responses are never
shared outside People Operations.**

This summary covers exits between 1 January and 30 June 2026. It combines
the exit interview survey sent to every leaver with the optional
face-to-face conversation offered by People Operations. Figures are for
voluntary exits only unless stated.

## Headline numbers

- Total exits: 112, of which 94 were voluntary.
- Exit interviews completed: 79 of the 94 voluntary leavers, a response rate
  of 84 percent.
- Annualised voluntary attrition: 13.6 percent, up from 11.9 percent in H2
  2025.
- Regretted leavers (rated 4 or 5 in the last review): 41, or 44 percent of
  voluntary exits.
- Median tenure at exit: 2.3 years.

## Attrition by department

| Department | Voluntary exits | Annualised attrition |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering | 46 | 19.2 percent |
| Finance | 14 | 11.8 percent |
| HR | 6 | 9.4 percent |
| IT | 12 | 10.1 percent |
| Facilities | 5 | 6.7 percent |
| Legal | 3 | 8.0 percent |
| Other | 8 | 9.9 percent |

## Main reasons for leaving

Leavers could choose one main reason.

1. Pay below market: 29 percent. Most of these were G3 and G4 engineers who
   left for offers 30 to 45 percent higher.
2. Limited career progression: 23 percent.
3. Relationship with manager: 16 percent. Two teams in Engineering
   accounted for nine of these responses.
4. Relocation or family reasons: 12 percent.
5. Workload and on-call burden: 11 percent.
6. Other: 9 percent.

## Where leavers went

58 percent joined product companies, 21 percent went to large IT services
firms, 9 percent moved to startups and the rest left the workforce or did
not say. Three leavers joined the same direct competitor in the payments
space; the HR Business Partner has shared this with the Engineering
leadership team.

## Would they return?

62 percent said they would consider coming back. Regretted leavers who
said yes are added to the alumni re-hire list, which Talent Acquisition
reviews every quarter.

## Actions agreed

- Total Rewards to bring forward the market review of the G3 and G4
  Engineering bands.
- HR Business Partners to run skip-level conversations in the two
  Engineering teams named in the manager feedback.
- Site Reliability to review the on-call rota load before the Q4 rota is
  finalised.
