# How to Connect to Office Wi-Fi

Every office has two wireless networks.

- **CORP:** for company laptops only. Gives full access to internal systems
  without the VPN.
- **GUEST:** for visitors and personal phones. Internet access only.

## Company laptops
Company laptops join CORP automatically using a device certificate. If the
laptop does not connect, restart it while in the office so it can renew the
certificate.

## Personal phones
Select GUEST and accept the terms on the sign-in page. The guest password is
displayed at reception.

## Still not working?
Raise a ticket in the IT service portal with your office, floor and the
network name you tried.
