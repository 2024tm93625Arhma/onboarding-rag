# Payroll FAQ

Answers to the questions Payroll is asked most often. For this year's exact
dates, see the Payroll Calendar.

## Getting paid

**When is salary paid?**
On the last working day of each month. Payslips are published in the HR
system on the same day.

**What is the payroll cut-off?**
The 20th of each month. Anything that changes your pay has to be approved by
then to be included in that month's salary. Later approvals are processed in
the following month.

**I joined after the cut-off. Will I be paid this month?**
No. Your salary for the days you worked in your joining month is paid with the
next month's salary, together with that month's pay. Your first payslip shows
both amounts as separate lines.

**My salary has not arrived. What should I do?**
First check your payslip in the HR system. If it shows the salary as paid,
the credit may be delayed by your bank; wait until the end of the next
working day. If it still has not arrived, raise an urgent ticket with Payroll
through the HR helpdesk and attach the payslip.

**How do I change the bank account my salary goes to?**
See How to Change Your Salary Bank Account. The change must be approved
before the cut-off to take effect that month.

## Your payslip

**How do I download my payslip?**
See How to Download Your Payslip.

**Why is my take-home pay different this month?**
Common reasons are an expense reimbursement, a bonus, arrears from a salary
revision, leave without pay, a change in tax deduction after you updated your
investment declaration, or a new deduction such as a parking permit or an
insurance premium. Each item has its own line on the payslip.

**Where do expense reimbursements appear?**
As a separate, non-taxable line. They are paid in the first salary run after
your claim is approved, as long as approval happens before the cut-off. See
the Expense Claims Policy for the rest of the rules.

**Where are bonuses and referral payments shown?**
As separate earnings lines in the month they are paid. The amounts and
eligibility rules are in the relevant HR policy.

## Tax

**How is income tax worked out?**
Payroll deducts tax each month based on your expected annual income and the
tax regime you chose. If you do not choose, the default regime set by the
Income Tax Department is used.

**When do I declare my investments?**
In April, in the HR system under *Pay* > *Tax Declaration*. You can update it
during the year; changes approved before the cut-off affect that month's
deduction.

**When do I submit proofs?**
Payroll opens the proof submission window in December and closes it at the
January cut-off. Declarations without proof are removed, and any extra tax is
deducted from the remaining salaries of the financial year.

**When do I get Form 16?**
By 15 June each year, for the previous financial year. It is published in the
HR system under *Pay* > *Tax Documents*.

**I joined mid-year. What do I do about my previous employer's income?**
Upload the Form 12B or payslips from your previous employer in the HR system
so tax is worked out on your full-year income. If you do not, you may owe tax
when you file your return.

## Provident fund

**Is PF deducted from my salary?**
Yes, for eligible employees. Your contribution and the company's are shown on
the payslip.

**How do I find my UAN?**
It is shown on your payslip. If you had a UAN with a previous employer, give
it to Payroll during onboarding so your accounts are linked.

## Leaving the company

**When is my final settlement paid?**
Your full and final settlement is processed once exit clearance is complete.
It includes salary up to your last day, leave encashment where the Annual
Leave Policy allows it, and any other amounts due, less any recoveries. You
receive a settlement statement before it is paid.

**Will I still get my Form 16?**
Yes. It is published by 15 June in the usual way. Make sure your personal
email address is up to date in the HR system before you leave.

## Contact

**Who do I ask about something not covered here?**
Raise a ticket with Payroll through the HR helpdesk. Do not send bank details
or tax documents by email or chat; upload them in the HR system.
