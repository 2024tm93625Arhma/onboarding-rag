# Probation Policy (v1)

## Purpose
This policy describes the probation period for new employees and how it is
reviewed and confirmed.

## Probation period
Every new employee serves a probation period of **6 months** starting on
their date of joining. During probation the employee and manager agree clear
objectives in the first two weeks and hold a documented check-in at the
midpoint of the probation period.

## Confirmation
At the end of probation the manager completes a confirmation review in the HR
system. The outcome is one of: confirmed, extended, or not confirmed. HR sends
a confirmation letter to the employee once the review is approved.

## Extension
Probation may be extended once, for a period agreed between the manager and
HR, where there is a clear performance or conduct concern that the employee has
a fair chance to address. The reasons must be recorded in writing and shared
with the employee.

## Notice during probation
The notice period during probation is shorter than after confirmation; it is
stated in the employee's offer letter.

## Benefits during probation
Employees on probation are eligible for statutory benefits and group health
insurance from their date of joining.

## Applicability
This policy applies to employees who joined before 1 July 2026.
