# Compensation Review Cycle 2026

**Classification: Confidential. Managers at G6 and above, and HR.**

This document explains how managers recommend pay changes in the 2026
compensation review, which follows the calibration of year-end ratings. It
covers the budget, the merit matrix and the rules for exceptions. The dates
are in Compensation Review 2026: Key Dates.

## Budget

- **Merit pool:** 8.5 percent of the fixed payroll of eligible employees.
- **Promotion pool:** 1.5 percent of the fixed payroll, held centrally by
  Total Rewards.
- **Market adjustment pool:** 0.75 percent, held centrally, for employees
  well below their band after the 2026 benchmark.

Each manager receives a merit budget in the HR system equal to 8.5 percent
of their team's fixed payroll. Managers may move money between employees
but may not exceed the team total.

## Eligibility

Employees who joined on or before 31 December 2025 and have a final rating
are eligible. Those who joined later receive no merit increase this cycle.
Red-circled employees receive a lump sum instead of a fixed-pay increase.

## Merit matrix

The guideline increase depends on the final rating and on where the
employee sits in their band (compa-ratio).

| Rating | Compa-ratio below 0.90 | 0.90 to 1.10 | Above 1.10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 16 to 20 percent | 13 to 16 percent | 9 to 12 percent |
| 4 | 11 to 14 percent | 9 to 11 percent | 6 to 8 percent |
| 3 | 7 to 9 percent | 5 to 7 percent | 3 to 5 percent |
| 2 | 0 to 3 percent | 0 to 2 percent | 0 percent |
| 1 | 0 percent | 0 percent | 0 percent |

Recommendations outside the guideline range need a written reason and the
HR Business Partner's approval.

## Variable pay multiplier

Variable pay is the target percentage for the grade multiplied by the
rating multiplier and the company scorecard result. For FY 2025-26 the
scorecard closed at 92 percent.

| Rating | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| 5 | 1.5 |
| 4 | 1.2 |
| 3 | 1.0 |
| 2 | 0.5 |
| 1 | 0 |

## Lump sums for red-circled employees

Red-circled employees with a rating of 3 or above receive a one-time lump
sum equal to half of the increase they would otherwise have received. The
lump sum is not added to fixed pay.

## Communicating the outcome

Do not tell employees their increase until the letters are released.
Discuss only the employee's own outcome, never the budget, the matrix or
anyone else's increase.
