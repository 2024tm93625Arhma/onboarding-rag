# Checklist for Onboarding a New Vendor

Use this checklist when you want to buy from a supplier who is not yet set up
in the procurement system. You are the *requester*: the employee who wants to
use the vendor. The full process is in the Vendor Onboarding SOP.

## Before you ask
- [ ] Search the procurement system to confirm the vendor is not already set
      up, perhaps under a different trading name.
- [ ] Check with Procurement whether an existing approved vendor can do the
      same work.
- [ ] Get your budget holder's agreement to use a new vendor.

## Raise the request
- [ ] Open the procurement system and select *Vendors* > *New Vendor
      Request*.
- [ ] Enter the vendor's legal name, contact person and what you plan to buy.
- [ ] Enter an estimate of annual spend.
- [ ] Submit. The system emails the vendor a link to the supplier portal.

## Documents the vendor uploads
- [ ] PAN card
- [ ] GST registration certificate, or a declaration that they are not
      registered
- [ ] Cancelled cheque or bank letter confirming the account details
- [ ] MSME (Udyam) registration certificate, if they have one
- [ ] Signed Supplier Code of Conduct
- [ ] For vendors who will handle company data: a completed information
      security questionnaire

## While you wait
- [ ] Chase the vendor if they have not uploaded documents within a few days.
      Procurement cannot start checks until everything is in.
- [ ] Do not place an order or let work begin. Work started before the vendor
      is approved and a PO is issued may not be paid.

## When the vendor is approved
- [ ] You receive a notification with the vendor number.
- [ ] Raise your purchase requisition; see How to Raise a Purchase Order.

Questions go to Procurement through a Finance ticket on the employee portal.
