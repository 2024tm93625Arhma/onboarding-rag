# Facilities Help Desk Contacts

The Facilities help desk handles anything to do with the office itself:
desks, lockers, meeting rooms, parking permits, cleaning, repairs and
building access.

## How to reach us
- **Employee portal:** raise a ticket under *Facilities*. This is the best
  route for anything that is not urgent.
- **Chat:** post in the #ask-facilities channel for quick questions.
- **In person:** the Facilities desk is next to reception on the ground floor
  of each office.

## Urgent issues
For a water leak, a power failure or anything unsafe, call the Facilities
duty line on internal extension 2200. For a medical emergency or fire, call
Security on extension 2222.

## Not Facilities?
Laptop, Wi-Fi and printer problems go to the IT service desk. Pay and leave
questions go to the HR helpdesk.
