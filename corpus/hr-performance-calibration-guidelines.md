# Performance Calibration Guidelines for Managers

**Classification: Confidential. Managers at G6 and above, and HR.**

These guidelines tell managers how to prepare for and run the calibration
step of the annual performance review. The Performance Review Guide
describes the cycle for everyone. This document covers the parts that are
shared only with people managers: the rating scale, the target distribution
and the rules the calibration committee applies.

## The rating scale

| Rating | Label |
|---|---|
| 5 | Exceptional |
| 4 | Exceeds expectations |
| 3 | Meets expectations |
| 2 | Partially meets expectations |
| 1 | Does not meet expectations |

## Target distribution

Each calibration group is expected to land close to this distribution. It
is a guide, not a forced curve, but any group that is more than 5 points
away from the target for a rating must explain why in the calibration notes.

- Rating 5: up to 10 percent
- Rating 4: 20 to 25 percent
- Rating 3: 50 to 60 percent
- Rating 2: 8 to 12 percent
- Rating 1: up to 5 percent

## Calibration groups

A calibration group is a department, or a part of a department, with at
least 15 rated employees. Smaller teams are pooled with a related team so
that the distribution means something. Each group is chaired by the
department head, with the HR Business Partner as facilitator. A session is
valid only if at least two thirds of the managers in the group attend.

## Rules the committee applies

1. A rating of 5 needs at least two concrete examples of impact beyond the
   employee's own team, and must be supported by a second manager in the
   room.
2. A rating of 1 is allowed only if the employee was told in writing about
   the performance gap at least 90 days before the end of the review year.
   If that did not happen, the lowest rating the committee can give is 2.
3. A rating of 2 or 1 for two cycles in a row triggers a performance
   improvement plan, run with the HR Business Partner.
4. Managers may not calibrate their own ratings upward after the session.
   Any change after calibration needs the department head and the HR
   Business Partner to sign off.

## Promotion nominations

Promotions are discussed in the same session. An employee can be nominated
only if they have a rating of 4 or 5 in the current cycle and have spent at
least 18 months in their current grade. Nominations for G6 and above are
reviewed again by the executive team.

## Confidentiality

Do not share the target distribution, other employees' ratings or the
discussion in the room with your team. Tell each employee only their own
final rating and the reasons for it.
