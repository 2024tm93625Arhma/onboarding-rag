# Accounts Payable Team Wiki - Employee Claims

How the AP team processes employee expense claims. New team members should
read this in their first week.

## Where claims arrive
Employees submit claims in the expense tool. Claims appear in the AP queue
after the employee's manager has approved them.

## Checks before paying
- The receipt is attached and legible.
- The amount and date on the receipt match the claim line.
- The cost centre is valid and belongs to the employee's team.
- The expense category matches what was bought.

## Late claims
Claims must be submitted within 60 calendar days of the date the expense was
incurred. Reject anything older with the reason *Outside submission window*
unless the claim carries a written exception from the Finance Controller.

## Returning a claim
If something is missing or wrong, return the claim to the employee with a
clear comment rather than rejecting it. Returned claims keep their original
submission date.

## Payment runs
Approved claims are paid in the next weekly payment run on Thursday. Claims
approved after 12:00 on Wednesday go into the following week's run.

## Escalations
Send disputed claims to the AP lead. Suspected fraud goes straight to the
Finance Controller and is not discussed with the employee.
