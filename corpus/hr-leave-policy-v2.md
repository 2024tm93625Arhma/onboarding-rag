# Annual Leave Policy (v2)

## Purpose
This policy sets out how annual leave is earned, used and carried forward.
It replaces v1 for employees who joined on or after 1 April 2025.

## Entitlement
Full-time employees earn 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
accrued monthly at 2 days per month.

## Carry-forward
Employees may carry forward up to **5 unused leave days** into the next
calendar year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March of that year,
or they expire.

## Applicability
This policy applies to employees who joined on or after 1 April 2025.
Employees who joined before that date remain under v1.
