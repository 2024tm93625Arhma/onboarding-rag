# Submitting Expenses in ClaimPoint

ClaimPoint is the system for claiming back money you have spent on company
business. This guide explains how to fill in a claim form in ClaimPoint and
what happens after you submit it. What you may claim is set by the Expense
Claims Policy.

## Logging in
Open ClaimPoint from the Finance section of the intranet. It only works on
the office network or through the Remote Desktop Gateway. Log in with your
employee number and your ClaimPoint password; this is separate from your
Windows password.

## Creating a claim
1. Click *New Claim* and choose the month the expenses belong to.
2. Add one line per expense. Choose the expense type from the drop-down list
   and enter the date, amount and a short description.
3. Enter your cost centre code. Ask your manager if you do not know it.
4. Click *Save and Print*. ClaimPoint prints a claim form with a barcode.

## Sending the paper receipts
ClaimPoint does not store receipts. Staple your original paper receipts to
the printed claim form, sign it and get your manager to sign it too. Put it
in the internal mail envelope marked *Finance - Claims* and drop it in the
tray at reception.

## After you submit
Finance scans the barcode when the envelope arrives, checks the receipts and
approves the claim. The status in ClaimPoint changes from *Printed* to
*Received* and then to *Approved*. Approved claims are paid with your salary.

## Common problems
- **Claim stuck at Printed:** Finance has not received the paper form. Check
  that you dropped it at reception.
- **Forgot ClaimPoint password:** click *Reset password*; a new one is sent
  to your email.
- **Wrong cost centre:** ask Finance to reject the claim, then create a new
  one.
