# Manager Checklist for New Joiners

A good start makes a lasting difference. Use this checklist when someone joins
your team.

## Before they start
- [ ] Raise a laptop request through the IT service portal as described in
      the Laptop Request and Refresh Policy.
- [ ] Request access to the tools and shared drives your team uses.
- [ ] Choose an onboarding buddy from your team and brief them.
- [ ] Send a welcome email with the start time, office location and the
      buddy's name.
- [ ] Block time in your calendar for the first day.

## First day
- [ ] Meet your new joiner in person or on video.
- [ ] Introduce them to the team and key stakeholders.
- [ ] Walk them through the team's goals and current projects.
- [ ] Explain your team's anchor days and how the team uses the Work From Home
      Policy; point them to the version that applies to their join date.

## First weeks
- [ ] Agree written objectives for the probation period. The timing and review
      steps are in the Probation Policy.
- [ ] Hold regular one-to-ones and ask what is and is not working.
- [ ] Make sure they have completed mandatory training in the learning
      portal.
- [ ] Check that their first expense claim, if any, is submitted correctly.

## End of probation
- [ ] Complete the confirmation review in the HR system on time. See the
      Probation Policy for the length of probation, which depends on the
      employee's join date.

## Tips
Avoid relying on your own experience of policies: several HR policies differ
depending on when an employee joined. When in doubt, check the applicability
section of the policy or ask People Operations.
