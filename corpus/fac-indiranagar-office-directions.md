# Getting to the Indiranagar Office

Our Bengaluru office is at 4th Floor, Maple Court, 12th Main Road,
HAL 2nd Stage, Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560038. This page explains how to get
there and what to do when you arrive for the first time.

## By metro
Take the Purple Line to Indiranagar station. Leave by the exit towards
100 Feet Road and walk south for about ten minutes, then turn left onto
12th Main Road. Maple Court is the grey building on the right, opposite the
bakery.

## By road
Most cab and auto drivers know Maple Court. If not, ask for "12th Main,
HAL 2nd Stage, near the Indiranagar police station". Drop-off is at the
front gate; cars cannot wait on 12th Main Road.

## Two-wheelers and cars
There is a small covered area for two-wheelers behind the building. The
building has only a few car spaces, and Facilities decides who gets them.
Street parking nearby is limited, so we recommend the metro.

## When you arrive
1. Sign in with the building security guard on the ground floor.
2. Take the lift to the 4th floor. Our reception is on the left as you
   leave the lift.
3. Reception will give you a temporary badge until your ID badge is ready.

## Floor layout
The 4th floor has the open work area, three meeting rooms (Banyan, Neem and
Peepal), a pantry and the server room. The 5th floor terrace is used for
town halls and team events.

## Contact
For questions about the office, email the Facilities team or call the
reception desk on extension 400.
