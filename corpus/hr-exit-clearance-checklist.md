# Exit Clearance Checklist

Use this checklist once your resignation has been accepted. Your relieving
letter and full and final settlement are issued only after every item is
cleared.

## Before your last working day
- [ ] Agree your last working day with your manager and confirm it in the HR
      system.
- [ ] Hand over your work: write a handover note, transfer document ownership
      and introduce your replacement or manager to your key contacts.
- [ ] Submit any outstanding expense claims within the deadline in the
      Expense Claims Policy. Claims raised after you leave cannot be
      processed.
- [ ] Apply for or cancel any pending leave so that your balance is final.
- [ ] Settle any open advances with Finance.
- [ ] Complete the exit interview survey sent by HR.

## On your last working day
- [ ] Return your laptop, charger and any other company equipment. The return
      deadline and process are in the IT Asset Return Policy.
- [ ] Return your ID badge and any access cards to reception.
- [ ] Return your parking permit if you have one, as described in the Office
      Parking Policy.
- [ ] Remove personal files from your laptop. Do not copy company data to
      personal devices.
- [ ] Update your personal email address in the HR system so that we can send
      your documents.

## After you leave
- [ ] Watch for your relieving letter and experience letter by email.
- [ ] Check your full and final settlement statement and raise any questions
      with Payroll through the HR helpdesk.

## For managers
Confirm in the HR system that the handover is complete and that no company
equipment remains with the employee. Ask IT to revoke access on the last
working day.

Questions about exits can be raised with People Operations through the HR
helpdesk.
