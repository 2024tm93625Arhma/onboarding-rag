# How to Request an NDA

Request a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) before you share any confidential
information with a company outside the group.

1. Open the Legal portal and choose **Request an NDA**.
2. Pick the template: **mutual** if both sides will share information,
   **one-way** if only we will.
3. Enter the other party's full legal name and registered address. Copy
   these from their website or invoice, not from an email signature.
4. Describe in one or two sentences why you need to share information.
5. Submit the request.

Legal fills in the template and sends it for e-signature. You will get an
email when the NDA is signed. Until then, share only public information.
