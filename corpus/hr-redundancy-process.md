# Redundancy Process

**Classification: Restricted. HR only.**

This process applies when a role, or a group of roles, is no longer needed
because of a restructure, a closed business line or a site consolidation. It
is run by Employee Relations with the HR Business Partner for the affected
department. Nothing in this document may be discussed with employees until
the announcement step.

## Approvals

- Up to 10 roles: department head and the Chief People Officer.
- 11 to 50 roles: the Chief People Officer and the Chief Financial Officer.
- More than 50 roles, or any closure of an office: the Board.

## Selection

Where only some roles in a pool are affected, employees are scored on a
selection matrix: skills needed for the future structure (40 percent),
performance ratings from the last two cycles (35 percent), and
disciplinary record (25 percent). Length of service is used only as a tie
break. Employees on maternity leave, on long medical leave or who have
raised a grievance in the last twelve months cannot be selected without a
review by Legal.

## Consultation

Affected employees get a 45-day consultation period, starting on the day
they are told. During this period they can suggest alternatives, apply for
open roles internally and bring a colleague to every meeting. Employees who
find an internal role during consultation move without a trial period.

## Severance package

The company pays more than the statutory minimum:

- **Severance pay:** 30 days of basic pay for every completed year of
  service, with a floor of 90 days of basic pay and a cap of 24 months of
  basic pay.
- **Notice pay:** paid in lieu of the full contractual notice.
- **Medical cover:** group medical cover for the employee and dependants
  continues for 180 days after the exit date.
- **Outplacement:** a career transition service for 120 days, including CV
  support and interview coaching.
- **Leave:** all unused earned leave is encashed, whatever the normal cap.

The package is paid only after the employee signs a separation agreement,
which Legal drafts. Employees are given 10 working days to review it.

## Announcement

Affected employees are told in a one-to-one meeting with their manager and
the HR Business Partner, followed by a written letter the same day. Team
announcements happen only after every affected employee has been told.

## Re-hiring

A role made redundant cannot be re-advertised for nine months. If it is,
the former employee must be offered it first.
