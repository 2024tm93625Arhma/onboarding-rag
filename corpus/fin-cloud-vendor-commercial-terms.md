# Cloud Vendor Commercial Terms

**Classification: Confidential. Finance only. The pricing terms are covered
by a confidentiality clause in the agreement.**

This page summarises the commercial terms of the company's main cloud
hosting agreement with Nimbus Cloud Services, so that FP&A, Procurement and
Accounts Payable can plan, check invoices and track the commitment. The
signed agreement is in the contract register under reference
CR-2025-0417.

## Commitment

- **Term:** three years, from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028.
- **Total committed spend:** INR 270 crore over the term.
- **Annual minimums:** INR 78 crore in year one, INR 90 crore in year two
  and INR 102 crore in year three.
- **Shortfall:** if spend in a year is below that year's minimum, Nimbus
  invoices 60 percent of the shortfall. A shortfall cannot be carried into
  the next year.
- **Overspend:** spend above the annual minimum counts towards the next
  year's minimum, up to INR 10 crore.

## Discounts

- A flat discount of 19 percent on list prices for compute, storage and
  networking.
- An extra 6 percent on managed database services.
- Reserved capacity bought through the agreement keeps its own discount but
  still counts in full towards the commitment.
- Marketplace purchases count towards the commitment only up to 15 percent
  of the annual minimum.

## Credits and support

- **Migration credits:** INR 4.5 crore, usable only in year one. INR 3.8
  crore was used; the rest has lapsed.
- **Support:** enterprise support is included at no charge, with a named
  technical account manager.
- **Training:** 40 certification vouchers a year for Engineering staff.

## Invoicing

Nimbus invoices monthly in INR, in arrears, with GST charged separately. The
invoice goes to Accounts Payable against the standing purchase order
PO-CLOUD-2025. Engineering - Platform checks the usage report before AP
approves payment. Any line more than 10 percent above the previous month
needs Platform's written confirmation.

## Tracking

FP&A reports spend against the annual minimum every month. At the end of Q1
of year two, spend was on track to finish the year at INR 96 crore, INR 6
crore above the minimum.

## Renewal

Notice of renewal or non-renewal must be given by 30 September 2027.
Procurement will begin benchmarking alternatives in April 2027. Any early
conversation with Nimbus about renewal must go through Procurement.
