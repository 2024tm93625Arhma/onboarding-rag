# Work From Home Policy (v2)

## Purpose
This policy sets out when employees may work from home, how to arrange it and
what is expected of them while working remotely. The company operates a hybrid
model: collaboration happens best in the office, while focused work can often
be done from home.
It replaces v1 for employees who joined on or after 1 January 2025.

## Scope
This policy covers full-time employees, contract employees and interns alike.
Contractors and interns follow the same rule as full-time employees; for them,
the join date is the start date of their current contract or internship.

## Allowance
Eligible employees may work from home for up to **2 days** per week.
Unused days do not carry over to the following week and cannot be combined
into a longer block of remote working.

## Anchor days
Each team agrees one or two anchor days per week on which everyone is expected
in the office. Work-from-home days should not be taken on a team's anchor days
unless the manager agrees in advance.

## How to arrange work from home
1. Mark the days in the attendance system by Friday of the previous week.
2. Tell your manager if you plan to change a day at short notice.
3. Keep your calendar status set to "Working remotely" on those days.

Managers may decline or reschedule a work-from-home day where the business
need requires the employee on site, for example for a client visit, an
audit or an incident review.

## Working hours and availability
Employees working from home keep the same core hours as in the office, 10:00
to 16:00 local time, and must be reachable on chat and phone during those
hours. Meetings should be joined with the camera on where practical.

## Workspace and equipment
Employees must work from a quiet, private space with a reliable internet
connection. Company laptops must be used for all work; personal devices may not
be used to access company systems. Remote access to internal systems must go
through the company VPN as described in the IT VPN Access policy.

## Security and confidentiality
Screens must be locked whenever the employee steps away. Confidential documents
should not be printed at home. Work must not be done from public places such
as cafes on work-from-home days unless the manager has agreed.

## Health and safety
Employees are responsible for setting up a safe and ergonomic workspace at
home. The company can provide a monitor and keyboard on request through the IT
service desk.

## Communication and performance
Working from home does not change what is expected of an employee. Managers
assess performance on outcomes, not on where the work is done. Teams should
agree how they share updates, for example through a short daily written
check-in, so that people working remotely are not left out of decisions made in
the office. Important decisions discussed in person should be written up in the
team channel on the same day.

## Temporary exceptions
Employees recovering from illness or injury, or with a temporary medical
condition, may be given additional work-from-home days on the advice of the
company doctor. Such exceptions are agreed with HR, recorded in the HR system
and reviewed at least monthly.

## Longer remote periods
Requests to work remotely for more than one consecutive week, for example to
care for a family member or while relocating, are handled as a temporary
remote-work arrangement. They need approval from the manager and the HR
business partner and are reviewed after one month.

## Working from another city or country
Working from a location other than the employee's registered home address
requires prior HR approval. Working from outside India is not permitted under
this policy because of tax and data-protection rules.

## Office days
On office days employees should book a desk through the desk booking system as
described in the Facilities Desk Booking policy.

## Applicability
This policy applies to employees who joined on or after 1 January 2025.
Employees who joined before that date remain under v1.

## Contact
Questions about this policy can be raised with the HR helpdesk through the
employee portal.
