# How to Report a Maintenance Issue

Report anything in the office that is broken, dirty or unsafe to the
Facilities help desk. Reporting it quickly helps everyone who uses the space.

## What to report
- Broken chairs, desks, standing frames or locker doors.
- Lights that are out or flickering.
- Rooms that are too hot or too cold.
- Blocked or leaking washrooms and pantries.
- Spills, pest sightings and cleaning problems.
- Damaged doors, windows, floor tiles or signage.

Monitors, docking stations, printers and meeting room screens are IT
equipment. Report those to the IT service desk instead.

## How to report it
1. Open the employee portal and raise a ticket under *Facilities >
   Maintenance*.
2. Choose the office, floor and area. Desk and room numbers are printed on a
   small label on each desk and door.
3. Describe the problem in one or two sentences.
4. Attach a photo if you can. It helps the technician bring the right parts.
5. Submit the ticket. You will get updates on the ticket as it is assigned and
   fixed.

## Urgent problems
Do not use a ticket for anything that is dangerous or getting worse, such as a
water leak, a burning smell, sparking sockets or a trapped lift. Call the
Facilities duty line on extension 2200. If anyone is hurt or there is a fire,
call Security on extension 2222 first.

## Marking an unsafe item
If a chair or desk is unsafe, put a *Do not use* tag on it. Tags are kept at
the Facilities desk and in each pantry. Do not try to repair furniture or
electrical fittings yourself.

## Following up
If a ticket has had no update for longer than you expect, reply on the ticket
rather than opening a new one. The Facilities Escalation Contacts page shows
who to go to next.
