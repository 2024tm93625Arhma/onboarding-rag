# ID Badge and Office Access FAQ

**Where do I get my ID badge?**
New joiners collect their badge from reception on their first day. Bring a
government photo ID. Your photo is taken at the desk if one was not uploaded
during onboarding.

**What does the badge open?**
The main entrance, the floors your team works on, the pantries and your
locker if it has a badge reader. Restricted areas such as server rooms, the
mail room and the Security office need separate approval.

**How do I get access to another floor or a restricted area?**
Raise a ticket under *Facilities > Access* and name the area and the reason.
Your manager approves the request. Restricted areas also need approval from
the area owner.

**I forgot my badge. Can I still get in?**
Yes. Go to reception with a photo ID. You will get a temporary day badge,
which must be handed in at reception before you leave. Repeatedly forgetting
your badge is reported to your manager.

**I lost my badge. What should I do?**
Report it straight away by calling Security on extension 2222 or raising an
urgent ticket under *Facilities > Access*. Security deactivates the lost badge
and arranges a replacement. A replacement for a lost badge may be charged; the
charge is shown on the ticket.

**My badge stopped working.**
Badges can fail if they are bent or kept next to a phone magnet. Ask reception
to test it. If it is faulty, reception issues a new one.

**Can I let someone in behind me?**
No. Everyone must tap their own badge. Do not hold doors open for people you
do not know, even if they look like employees. Visitors are signed in at
reception under the Visitor Pass Policy.

**Do I have to wear the badge?**
Yes. Wear it visibly on a lanyard or clip at all times in the office.

**What happens when I leave the company?**
Hand in your badge on your last working day as part of exit clearance. It is
deactivated at the end of that day.
