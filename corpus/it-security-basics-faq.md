# Security Basics FAQ for New Joiners

A short set of answers to the security questions new joiners ask in their
first week.

**What are the rules for my account password?**
Your password must be at least 12 characters long and must not contain your
name or username. A passphrase of several unrelated words is easier to
remember than a random string and just as strong.

**Can I reuse a password I use somewhere else?**
No. Your work password must be unique. Never use it for personal accounts or
other websites.

**Where should I store my passwords?**
In the company password manager. Do not write passwords down or keep them in
a document or spreadsheet.

**Why do I have to approve sign-ins on my phone?**
Multi-factor authentication stops someone who has guessed or stolen your
password from signing in. Only approve a prompt if you started the sign-in
yourself.

**I think I clicked a phishing link. What now?**
Report it straight away with the *Report phishing* button in your mail client
and raise a ticket with the Service Desk. Reporting early is never a problem.

**Can I use a USB stick to move files?**
No. Use approved cloud storage to share files. USB storage is blocked on
company laptops.

**Should I lock my laptop when I step away?**
Always. Press Windows+L on Windows or Ctrl+Cmd+Q on a Mac. Screens lock
automatically after a short period, but do not rely on that.

**Who do I ask about something not covered here?**
The IT Service Desk.
