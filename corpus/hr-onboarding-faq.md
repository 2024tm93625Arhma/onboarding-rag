# New Joiner FAQ

Answers to the questions new employees ask most often in their first weeks.
Where a topic is covered by a policy, this page points you to the policy
rather than repeating it, because some policies differ depending on when you
joined.

## How much annual leave do I get, and can I carry it over?
Entitlement and carry-forward rules are set out in the Annual Leave Policy.
Check which version applies to you: it depends on your join date.

## How long is my probation?
See the Probation Policy. The length of probation depends on your date of
joining, and your offer letter also states it.

## How often can I work from home?
The Work From Home Policy sets the weekly allowance and how to book remote
days. The allowance depends on your join date, so read the version that
applies to you.

## How do I claim back money I spent on company business?
Submit a claim in the expense tool. The deadline for submitting claims and the
receipts you need are in the Expense Claims Policy; spending limits are in the
Finance Reimbursement Limits policy.

## I know someone who would be great here. Is there a referral bonus?
Yes. Submit the referral through the referral portal. The bonus amount and
eligibility rules are in the Employee Referral Bonus Policy.

## When do I get paid?
Salaries are paid monthly. Your payslip is available in the HR system; see
*How to Download Your Payslip*.

## Who is my onboarding buddy?
Your manager assigns a buddy from your team before you start. Their name is in
your welcome email. If you have not heard from them, ask your manager.

## Where do I find the Code of Conduct?
In the employee portal under *Policies*. You need to acknowledge it in your
first week.

## How do I get a laptop and software?
IT hands over your laptop on your first day. Additional software is requested
through the IT service portal as described in the Software Installation
Policy.

## Who do I ask if my question is not answered here?
Raise a ticket with the HR helpdesk, or post a non-confidential question in
the #ask-hr channel.
