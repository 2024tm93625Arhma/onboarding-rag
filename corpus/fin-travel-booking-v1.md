# Business Travel Booking Policy (v1)

## Purpose
This policy explains how business travel is approved and booked, so that trips
are safe, cost-effective and properly recorded.

## Approval before booking
All business travel needs approval from the employee's manager before anything
is booked. International travel also needs approval from the department head.
The travel request is raised in the travel portal and must state the purpose of
the trip, the dates, the destination and the estimated cost.

## Booking channel
All flights, trains and hotels must be booked through the company travel portal
or the Travel Desk. Bookings made directly with airlines, hotels or other
websites are not reimbursed unless the Travel Desk has confirmed in writing
that it could not make the booking.

## Advance booking
Domestic flights must be booked at least **14 days** before the travel
date. International flights should be booked as early as possible once the trip
is approved. Where a trip is arranged at short notice for a genuine business
reason, such as a client escalation, the employee's manager must record the
reason in the travel request; the Travel Desk will then book the best
available fare.

## Class of travel
- Domestic flights: economy class for all employees.
- International flights of more than eight hours: premium economy for
  employees at director level and above; economy for everyone else.
- Trains: AC two-tier or equivalent.

Upgrades paid by the company are not permitted. Employees may upgrade at their
own cost or with their own loyalty points.

## Hotels
The Travel Desk books hotels from the company's preferred list where one is
available in the destination city. Nightly hotel limits are set in the Finance
Reimbursement Limits policy. Stays with friends or family are allowed, but no
hotel allowance is paid in that case.

## Ground transport
Airport transfers and local travel at the destination should use company-
approved taxi services where available. Car rental needs prior approval from
the manager and is allowed only when it is cheaper or safer than alternatives.

## Changes and cancellations
Changes to a booked trip must be made through the Travel Desk. Fees caused by
changes for personal reasons are paid by the employee. If a trip is cancelled,
the employee must cancel all bookings as soon as possible to reduce charges.

## Travel safety
Employees travelling internationally must register the trip with the Travel
Desk, carry the company travel insurance card and follow any travel advisories
issued by the Security team. Travel to countries on the company's restricted
list is not allowed without approval from the Chief Operating Officer.

## Passports and visas
Employees are responsible for holding a valid passport with at least six months
of validity remaining on the date of return. The Travel Desk arranges business
visas and the company pays visa fees for approved trips. Visa applications
should be started as soon as the trip is approved, since processing times vary
by country.

## Travel advances
Employees may request a cash or forex advance for international trips through
the travel portal. Advances must be settled in the expense tool after the trip,
and any unused amount is returned to the company or deducted from salary.

## Combining business and personal travel
Personal days may be added to a business trip with the manager's approval. Any
extra cost caused by the personal portion, such as a higher fare or additional
hotel nights, is paid by the employee, and the company's travel insurance does
not cover the personal days.

## Expenses during travel
Meals, local conveyance and other costs during the trip are claimed through the
expense tool under the HR Expense Claims policy, within the limits of the
Reimbursement Limits policy.

## Applicability
This policy applies to all employees.

## Contact
Contact the Travel Desk through the travel portal or the Finance helpdesk.
